## Decision and the next scientific gate

**No evolutionary improvement has been demonstrated.** Native feasibility remains
UNKNOWN. This identifies a plausible short path in an explicitly biased compact
representation, while showing why a path alone is insufficient. The clearest
remaining gap is actual within-life affordability and selection, including the
decoder overhead that can consume the whole projected margin.

Before a finite evolutionary pilot: qualify a forward lifetime ledger that
charges before actions, forbids spending future food, includes decoder/copy/state
formation and startup transfers without double charging, retains early deaths
and failed copies, and demonstrates blind-founder access to an earned division.
Then independently register fresh contexts, ordinary mutation, blank descendants,
no-mutation/inheritance/experience controls, matched simple strategies, all-member
denominators, finite limits and stopping rules. Admission remains separate;
