## Architectural decision

Do not replace the27-word Ora VM with this table architecture on this evidence.
It would reproduce a conventional one-bit strategy without a demonstrated net
advantage at the predeclared price. Native source already permits a six-dispatch
identity FORAGE/READ/XOR/CONST/XOR/FORAGE that can infer the next port from its own
attempt and success under matched site/phase. That is a source algebra identity,
not an evolved nine-slot reproductive genome or a qualified ecological path.
Native feasibility remains UNKNOWN; formal populations remain NO_GO.

The highest-value next experimental gate is a separately reviewed finite test
of **ordinary mutation plus actual paid reproduction in a recurring-information
ecology**, with blind founders, complete failed-lineage denominators, strong
reactive references, memory interventions and blank newborn state. Freeze the
affordability/opportunity distribution before evolutionary outcomes; do not
insert table2578 or raise rewards in this closed run. First resolve whether the
existing native ecology supplies a reusable informative opportunity that repays
its real replication costs. This census is a cheap prefilter, not that test.

Earlier evidence already warns against equating accuracy with fitness:
Learning81's supplied learner had scoped own-history choice success but failed
retained-energy superiority; Inheritance83's registered inherited-selection
criteria failed; Context90 supplied retrieval did not establish robust paid
advantage. These original conclusions are unchanged.

Primary research also distinguishes genotype-neighborhood accessibility from
actual adaptive population dynamics ([Kumawat et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11725885/)).
Results described as evolved learning may start with supplied learning machinery:
[Arnold et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12631) starts with A2C and an evolvable
neuromodulatory mechanism. This prototype makes no novelty claim over that work.
