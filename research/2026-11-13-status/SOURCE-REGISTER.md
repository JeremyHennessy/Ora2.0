## Evidence tiers

1. **Published primary research / software:** a remotely accessible article, source repository, code/configuration or official documentation. A published claim is not independently reproduced merely by reading it.
2. **GitHub publication independently opened:** this record or a research-only commit actually exists at the indicated URL.
3. **Owner-reported independently reviewed local experiment:** a detailed Codex report with named local commit and independent local verifier, recorded in issue #328. The issue is remotely readable; its referenced Windows checkout/receipts were not independently accessed during this publication. Never silently upgrade it to tier 2 or independent external replication.
4. **Research decision / proposal:** a reviewed design, accounting argument, or NO-GO. Not proof of a living organism, scientific evolution run, or measured campaign capacity.

- [Lenski et al. 2003, *The Evolutionary Origin of Complex Features*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568): prior peer-reviewed evolution of composed logic tasks under authored selection. These do not provide all original individual-birth receipts.
- [Avida official source](https://github.com/devosoft/avida): inspection can establish what a specific version/configuration *can* record, not which original 2003 records survived.
- [Avida data converters and example .spop archives](https://github.com/alife-data-standards/converters-avida): examples demonstrate genus/genotype lineage conversion but are **not** the original Lenski 2003 experiment.
- [Fontana & Buss, original AlChemy](https://sites.santafe.edu/~walter/Papers/tape.pdf) and [Return to AlChemy (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12137): relevant to compositional organization, initial-generator bias and trivial algebraic dynamics.
- [Bryson & Ofria 2013, virtual CPU architecture comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3871699/): expression power and evolutionary performance are task dependent.
