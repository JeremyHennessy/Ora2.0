## The findings that matter

### AL02 — paid hereditary reproduction and resource contingency (completed historical science)

The owner-reported continuation comprised **120 registered matched contexts** (distinct from eight earlier pilot contexts): accessible B food 880 paid births versus sequestered B 558, mean paired difference +2.683; 26 positive pairs, 94 ties, no negatives. B uptake occurred in 43 contexts, but 17 of those tied: resource consumption alone did not guarantee reproduction. All finite Worlds went extinct. These results concern the old bounded reproductive programs, not evolved intelligence.

### AL03 — genotype-by-resource reproductive effects (completed historical science)

Reported **eight jobs, 16 deterministic conditions, zero reruns**, four mutant/revertant B-use alleles and both founder orders. The registered resource-by-allele interactions were 18, 0, 12, 0; registered summary (J0 + J2)/2 = 15. Replacing COPY or DIVIDE with B use created B consumers with **no descendants**. A sterile B-consuming lineage reached the entire live population before extinction: survivor frequency is not reproductive selection. All Worlds became extinct. This is a same-background, externally transplanted mutation test with further mutation disabled; it does not demonstrate spontaneous spread of an allele in a new free-evolving population.

### Candidate B — bounded computationally native virtual programs (qualified components, no evolved task capability)

The [published architecture study](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/blob/82256ca2d06cdfb19e6b4471893f497e4819f4dc/research/substrate-decision-20261112/DECISION.md) compared the original restricted reproductive model, a general bounded program VM and compositional chemistry. Candidate B was selected as a **provisional scientific instrument**, not a winner across all possible substrates.

The original B-COMP-01 delayed-parity regime was **closed NO-GO**. In authored engineering fixtures, a timely memory program correctly answered 64/64 delayed probes and yielded 34–35 paid children with merit compared with 28–29 for its cue-storage-disabled counterpart. Nevertheless, a constant guesser reached 155 and compact copier 163 in their respective authored solo fixtures. Strong cheap alternatives and timing/computation/copy burdens made the chosen task incentive unsuitable as a memory-discovery calibration; no naturally evolved memory or competitive selection was demonstrated.

Shared-code reuse, partner-template replication and offspring self-repair were each explored only as bounded research/engineering concepts and then closed as currently unjustified. In the partner model, A paying to copy B produces a descendant inheriting **B**, not A. The old local copier required 31 charged units per cycle versus 30 for an optimistic external routine *before* lookup/provider/failure overhead. For an eight-word copier, an optimistic perfect-repair upper cost allowance was 2.764 charged units, below hypothetical inspection reads alone (16). These are **particular source-derived comparisons**, not impossibility proofs for all genomes, relatedness models or environments.

### Candidate C — resource-accounted compositional artificial chemistry (engineering qualified, collective heredity not demonstrated)

A bounded closed-expression normal-order reduction instrument, finite node/construction/traversal/substitution accounting and producer-independent DATA reconstruction were locally reported qualified: **14 test groups, 27 matching reaction histories, six matching readout histories**. An authored six-expression set regenerated members after single removal, but a constant/projection factory could explain the behavior; regeneration is not automatically functional organization.

A later **40-case** semantic-intervention qualification changed a real XOR truth table to XNOR, but found that constants/projections could produce the nominated target in four of four contexts—sometimes as an **intermediate expression**, even when the final Boolean result did not match. There was no scheduled turnover and no demonstrated heredity or selection.

The proposed four-parent/three-link externally mediated transfer assay was **closed before implementation** because its observers could not identify collective heredity. Different mixture compositions can have identical marginal response vectors; removing independent factories can falsely appear to establish interaction. A move-only transfer ledger is conceptually specified, **not implemented or qualified**. Candidate C's qualified chemistry is preserved as an instrument, but the present organizational-heredity/serial-transfer research branch is PARKED. No universal impossibility of artificial-chemistry heredity is claimed.

### OEB-01 — evolving simple and composed computation (prospective calibration, implementation paused)

This benchmark proposed inherited NOT, NAND and composed AND behavior in Candidate B, with explicit finite task merit 1/2/4/8 for zero to three whole-panel certificates. It did **not** grant a new scientific World. The benchmark design included eight matched selected/no-payoff seed blocks and required a composed-function genotype with earlier simple-function ancestry and three paid parent links. Only the research design and authored engineering controls were qualified.

Reported authored full-cost controls: compact copier 31 charged units/birth, NOT 43, NAND 43, AND 45, and all three 59. OEB integrated IO, merit scheduler and independent DATA path passed **seven bounded Jobs** and correctly refused three over-budget cases. With 1,024 basal opportunities, authored NOT with merit had 89 paid diagnostic children (3,849 charged work) versus identical NOT without merit 46 (2,018); compact copier had 78 (2,454). These are *not* equal-total-work competitive fitness and all children in these controls were unscheduled diagnostic descendants.

A final **eight-start** diagnostic allowance (no retries) showed complete 64-input assay success twice (about 5.0 child-Job CPU seconds each); two 4,096-step assay starts stopped at about 9.9 CPU seconds after 4,043/4,057 verified prefix instructions, respectively, and are **incomplete, not valid scientific zeros**. A complete assay retained ~1,072,806 raw bytes and 1,006,162 journal bytes. Across eight starts: 41.859 child-Job CPU seconds and all 16 child processes closed. The larger study's CPU and evidence requirements were not fully measured; a stated 10.27-hour/10.64-GB assay scenario is **conditional extrapolation**, not a forecast or hard limit.

**Decision C:** OEB-01's private population/evidence/ancestry infrastructure is disproportionate to a calibration already known in Avida-class systems. Preserve the working integrated instrument, original protocol, negative records and consumed claims; do not rerun or install a larger version merely to finish the calibration. **Candidate B is not thereby proved incapable of evolving useful computation.**

## Completed archive-first Avida audit (original plan preserved)

Focus on the source-authentic historical evidence behind Lenski et al. (2003), *The Evolutionary Origin of Complex Features*, and on Avida's **documented** native analysis capabilities. Published records describe the original 50-population study and 23 pivotal genotypes in a particular evolved-EQU lineage. Do not confuse 23 pivotal genotypes with 23 independent experimental populations.

1. Verify original genome/configuration and any available historic lineages or archived genotypes. Authenticate each file separately; **never** relabel modern example Avida data as 2003 originals.
2. Distinguish genotype ancestry / phylogenetic relationships, individual paid birth receipts, archived task phenotypes, mutation histories, and source-replayed execution. If an original file was not retained, say **UNKNOWN**.
3. Compare published Avida claims and available archives to Ora's independently checked producer/DATA model without assuming scientific or execution-assurance equivalence.
4. Evaluate whether any **new Ora-specific** research question truly needs our custom VM or whether a pinned Avida version could be a *future* comparative instrument. No Avida binary, evolved population, installation or new seed is authorized by this document.
5. Stop after one bounded reviewed archive assessment. Choose the next experiment from the real capability gap rather than another speculative "intelligence" feature.

## What would count as the next real breakthrough?

A new, naturally acquired functional program retained through paid descent, with strong inexpensive controls and measured reproductive effects; or a genuinely transmitted and varied functional organization in a different substrate. Published historical Avida findings establish precedent but are not Ora results.

**Not demonstrated:** an always-running evolving Ora universe, spontaneous memory learning, open-ended functional accumulation, autonomous organizational division, intelligence or consciousness.

**Hard gate:** Any future science requires an exact independently reviewed protocol, qualified code and DATA, actual measured resource envelopes, separately committed fresh contexts, explicit owner execution authority, and final independent GO. This summary itself authorizes none of those actions.
