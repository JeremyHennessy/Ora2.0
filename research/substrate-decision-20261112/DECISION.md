## What we actually know

The existing read-only AL03 analyzer was rerun against retained originals. It reproduced
8/8 jobs, 16/16 conditions, J=[18,0,12,0], primary `(J0+J2)/2=15`, no failed/missing jobs,
and extinction in every condition. Results match the committed analysis byte-for-byte.
The previous independent review remains `e47ee05c15828c9c85641f7afd0ab4d3c5b84b629143b54aca31b43c5379cf89`.
AL02's 120 continuation pairs and eight pilot pairs remain separate immutable baselines.

The important AL03 counterexamples are preserved. Removing COPY or DIVIDE yielded no
descendants despite B consumption; the COPY-loss lineage reached live frequency one
without reproducing. Revertant births did not decline when B became accessible. The
functional alleles gained an additional resource niche, not demonstrated efficiency,
learning, or suppressive competitive exclusion. Four positions share one ancestral
genetic background. AL03 used experimental transplants and disabled mutation; it was
not four independent instances of spontaneously spreading innovations.

Source inspection of `experiments/executed_heredity.py` establishes that the current
AL02/AL03 language has EAT_A, EAT_B, COPY, LOOP, DIVIDE and NOP. LOOP tests copy-head
completion and jumps to position 1; COPY substitutes symbols but does not insert/delete;
daughter length equals parent length. There is no general symbolic environmental input,
arbitrary data-dependent branch, register/RAM interface, or generic data operation.
PC, energy, nursery and heads ARE state: it would be wrong to say it processes no
information, or that six symbols mathematically cannot express computation. The actual
transition rules and reachable states, not the number six, impose the limitation.

Finite resources plus irreversible instruction costs explain eventual extinction in a
closed model. Extinction alone is not evidence that program evolution is impossible.
Likewise, extra food cannot provide an absent input/data-processing interface. Simply
adding food types, populations or generations does not address that architectural gap.

The founding charter leaves the substrate undecided, discloses authored constraints,
requires simpler controls, and rejects appearance/uptime as evidence of intelligence.
AI-Research EERC-06 separates physical possibility, mutational accessibility, selectable
net benefit and inherited function. We keep that distinction, not its untested combined
architecture. The 2026-10-23 EKA-0 decision rejects one authored ten-edit route as an
execution basis, not all computation or all possible learning routes. Older Mobile54 /
Renewable64 already describe registers, RAM, XOR, branching and SELFWRITE: computational
expressiveness is not a new Ora invention, and those parked proposals are not qualified
successes or new authority. Their larger scope is not imported wholesale.

## Three substrates compared without an invented overall score

| Criterion | A: current Ora | B: bounded programs | C: compositional artificial chemistry |
|---|---|---|---|
| New inherited functions | Demonstrated alternative food access; narrow action vocabulary | Generic operations compose new input/state/output functions; discovery unproved here | New catalytic/compositional networks possible; identifying functional heredity harder |
| Viable mutation neighborhood | Four-symbol ancestor often loses essential reproduction | Syntax-valid edits need not preserve reproduction; indels enlarge both opportunity and failure | Reaction closure can buffer variation or collapse; generator and compartment rules matter |
| Retaining/composing innovations | Exact genealogy excellent; fixed length and reproductive LOOP restrictive | Variable programs, data storage, relative branches; later routines possible, not guaranteed | Catalytic cooperation and collective reproduction; parasites and compositional dilution |
| Lifetime adaptation/transfer | No general interface; new experiment would change it | Volatile-state strategies possible; memory is not automatically learning | Distributed state/history possible; functional/generalization assays less direct |
| Sustained populations | Not in closed finite-food studies | CPU/memory replacement ecology can permit turnover; supplied scheduling/dilution must be disclosed | Flow/compartments can sustain reactions; stable soup is not necessarily an evolving lineage |
| Cheap-strategy/score exploitation | Sterile survival and niche access already exposed | Output spam, timing leaks, replication-only specialists, cheap reactive policies | Identity sinks, immortal complexes, abundance/entropy mistaken for innovation |
| Cost/scalability | Small and measured; narrow science | Cheap finite integer transitions, increasing copy/memory/log costs | Potentially expensive reduction/reaction search; spatial chemistry adds cost |
| Verification/containment | Mature exact DATA/custody baseline | Finite data-only interpreter and independent event replay are tractable | Reaction causality, group ancestry and nontermination cutoffs require more new machinery |

C is serious, not rejected for looking unfamiliar. A lambda-expression or Stringmol
soup can change interaction organization rather than merely execute a single genome.
It may eventually be better for endogenous construction and collective individuality.
It is presently a less economical instrument for the specific question of new useful
heritable computation: molecular closure, ownership, copying fidelity and group-level
causality would all have to be resolved simultaneously. A mass-conserving cellular field
is another credible C variant, but morphology, mass or visual novelty is not a substitute
for inherited function. No cross-substrate superiority experiment has been performed.

## Primary evidence and contrary cases

These are published author findings, not our independent replications. Existing local
E2 reviews were inspected; fresh primary pages/manuscripts were checked where available.

1. [Lenski et al. 2003](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01568): Avida demonstrates
   evolved composed logic under explicit task rewards and historical stepping stones.
   A fixed ISA does not preclude novel programs. This does not establish indefinite
   innovation or spontaneous goals; its environment explicitly selects functions.
2. [Bryson & Ofria 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3871699/): systematic
   virtual-CPU comparisons show architecture effects depend on task; extra memory and
   flexibility are not universal improvements. A larger language is not itself evidence.
3. [Grabowski et al. 2010, full paper](https://lalejini.com/mercere99.github.io/pubs/2010GrabowskiEtAl.pdf):
   memory-using digital behaviors and unseen-path performance evolved, but strong cheap
   nonmemory alternatives also arose. Cue/movement primitives and metabolic bonuses
   were authored. Our proposal is a local substrate falsifier, not field-first memory.
4. [Ray 1991](https://tomray.me/pubs/alife2/Ray1991AnApproachToTheSynthesisOfLife.pdf):
   CPU time and memory space can replace biological food metaphors. A supplied replicator
   and virtual machine are scaffolding, not an observed origin of replication.
5. [Egri-Nagy & Nehaniv 2003](https://uhra.herts.ac.uk/id/eprint/2526/1/101997.pdf):
   Physis allows inherited instruction definitions, yet processor structure stayed
   conservative and superior evolutionary potential was not established. Do not start
   with mutable interpreters merely because they are expressive.
6. [Clark et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5454285/): engineered
   Stringmol constructor/copier/genome systems underwent viable semantic changes, but
   incompatible expression cascades caused collapse. Fixed host rules still remained.
7. [Hickinbotham et al. 2021](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/176868/1/rsos.210441.pdf):
   spatial Stringmol produced complex parasite defenses, while 12/20 runs became extinct.
   Faster copying is not the only possible driver; surviving runs alone mislead.
8. [Fontana & Buss 1994](https://sites.santafe.edu/~walter/AlChemy/papers.html) and
   [Mathis et al. 2024 v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.12137v2): compositional organization
   is possible, but maintained ensembles are not automatically useful hereditary units.
   The reanalysis finds strong generator/standardization dependence and trivial sinks.
9. [Vasas et al. 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20080693/): a particular compositional
   genome model transmitted information too poorly for selectable fitter assemblies.
   This is a counterexample, not a proof against all chemistry.
10. [Taylor 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883): exploratory, expansive and transformational
    novelty are distinct. Universal expressiveness or an indefinitely running program
    alone does not establish open-ended innovation.
11. [Wilke et al. 2001](https://www.nature.com/articles/35085569) and
    [LaBar & Adami 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06299): mutation robustness and population
    size can change evolutionary routes. More mutations or stronger selection need not
    produce more useful complexity.

## Why B, and how small

Use a fixed verified host with bounded integer data and mutable variable-length programs.
The first instrument needs generic NAND, input/output, move/load/store, conditional and
relative branch, and executed paid copy/divide. No XOR task opcode, memory algorithm,
neural learner, automatic optimizer, subroutine library, self-modifying host, messaging,
evolving interpreter or world-construction API is needed. A tiny finite-state machine
can solve the first task: that is the correct simpler explanation and claim boundary.
The VM is chosen for reusable composition, explicit code inheritance and inspectable
costs, not because an FSM is incapable of the result. The experiment cannot establish
B's superiority over an evolving FSM or chemistry; that would require another comparison.

A small fixed instruction language can implement routines and virtual interpreters as
organism-owned data when its operations/memory suffice. Adding an inherited decoder
later could change the meaning of virtual instructions without changing host physics.
That is a possible future path, not evidence it will evolve or improve anything. All
finite configured machines here are finite-state; no claim of actual unbounded universal
computation. Self-modification adds copy/expression failure channels, so defer it until
ordinary composition proves useful. Host Python, eval/exec, shells, processes, files,
network, credentials, clocks, evidence and scheduler control are never organism operations.

Keep copying instructions mutable. Syntax-preserving fixed-width edits and relative
targets prevent parser crashes, not reproductive failure. No protected baby-making
kernel, imported solution, winner archive or manually selected rescue is hidden in B.
Initial viable replication remains a declared authored founder, not evolved abiogenesis.

## Acceleration: opportunities versus busy work

Independent populations improve the estimate of discovery probability and explore more
histories; larger populations increase mutation supply but can suppress drift or produce
clonal interference. Compare at equal **executed instructions**, births, evaluations and
host cost, not just elapsed time or population count. Parallelism only buys wall time.
No GPU/native rewrite is justified before a small instrument produces useful evidence.

For per-site substitution u and length L, faithful copying is approximately `(1-u)^L`
before indels/functional neutrality. At u=.01 this is .851 for L16 and .526 for L64:
longer programs add opportunities and error load/copy cost. Higher mutation is not a
free accelerator. Neutral padding, syntax-valid edits and small insertions/deletions
may create accessible stepping stones, but must be compared through functional descendants,
not celebrated as more diversity. Measure rather than assume viable-neighborhood size.

Environment variation can expose reusable strategies; too-fast change destroys retention,
and a complicated curriculum can author the solution. Start with one fixed, explicit
information opportunity and unseen sequences/delays. Preserve innovations through ordinary
copying and reproductive success, never by selecting interesting organisms for reinsertion.
Reusable code, ecological interactions and changing niches are later hypotheses, not a
feature list for this release.

## One next experiment and decision rule

See [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md): **B-COMP-01, evolved delayed parity under paid CPU selection**.
First cue x disappears, an unpredictable delay follows, then y appears. Correct response
is x XOR y, composed from generic operations. Balanced independent x makes every genuinely
history-independent strategy at most 50% accurate in expectation; this includes any
current-cue-only or periodic strategy. An authored one-bit finite-state controller solves
it, so success demonstrates useful finite-state computation, not learning a new rule.

The experiment compares task-coupled selection, no task payoff and parent-blind mutation
search across eight independent precommitted blocks. It retains entire endpoint censuses,
extinction, nulls and failures; held-out assays give no reproductive rewards. The primary
outcome is population prevalence of inherited, causally history-using useful programs,
not the best individual. Discovery alone need not beat random search: evolution is a
stochastic search process. The controls test the contribution of inherited selection,
not an impossible categorical distinction between evolution and randomness.

**Continue** if useful history-dependent functions arise and persist in multiple independent
lineages, confer paid reproductive benefit, and the paired population outcomes favor
selection over both controls under the fixed budget. **Pivot/stop** if random sampling or
cheap reactive strategies explain the outcomes, functional programs cannot pay their
costs, or repeated reproductive fragility prevents retention. A null at this budget
does not prove B cannot evolve computation. No ecological or learning claim follows.

The former finite-renewal proposal remains preserved as an ecological calibration, but
is not the next selected study: energy exhaustion is already analytically understood,
whereas programmable information use is the missing capability relevant to the charter.
CPU/memory scarcity and explicit replacement supply the first computational ecology;
they do not demonstrate autonomous self-maintenance or explain the origin of individuality.
