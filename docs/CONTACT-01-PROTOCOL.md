## Prospective extensions and matched nulls

Failed food contact: free reproduces ENERGY-02 eligibility pricing. Paid charges
up to2 usable units from the existing actor or external account when food/material
are available but capture/activation does not succeed (affinity mismatch or
insufficient processing work). Spend min(2,available work), dissipate exactly that
amount to heat; unavailable debit cannot be borrowed. Food is not consumed and
does not yield potential/work on failure. Compatible funded success still pays
only its existing2-unit price; do not double-charge. Unavailable food/material,
absent actor or a bound/already activated precursor costs0. Annotate unsuccessful
food contacts distinctly. Raw-template recognition already pays1 from the
precursor or uncoupled actor account, including mismatch; no extra fee there.
This is an alternative explicit contact law, not an inferred molecular price.

Loss round: loss0 is stable activation; loss1 removes the first available unit
from every raw precursor's local potential once, recording its unique ID and
converting it to heat. It cannot debit bonds/private work or synthesize replacement
activation. This is a discrete stability challenge, not an observed decay rate
or stochastic continuous kinetics. Do not tune loss, flux, prices or capacity
after outcomes. Quantitative natural-rate conclusions are outside this study.

Founder removal is a paired exogenous ablation, not monomer-origin generation:
at genesis retain C's immutable supplied birth history, disperse its B atoms to
ready inert material, dissipate n-1 supplied bond potential to heat, and sequester
its original work in an inaccessible labeled environment account. Remove active
C and its assembly channel. Total per-bit mass and total potential/work/heat
are identical to the present-founder fixture. No request can use the sequestered
account or turn those dispersed atoms into a polymer. External activation can
still operate, but absent biological actor cannot recognize/ligate/release.
Thus absent-founder failure is a structural missing-reaction control, not proof
that spontaneous origin is impossible in other substrates.

Ghost and untemplated controls retain ENERGY-02 semantics/costs. Ghost dissipation
is not functional inheritance. Untemplated actual bit order can preserve food

unit to have been captured/activated by that producer; externally activated
