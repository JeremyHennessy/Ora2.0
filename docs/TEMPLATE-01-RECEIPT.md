## Limits and decision

**Feasibility passed**, under supplied templates, ready reserves, matching finite
food and authored encounter schedules. Recognition, copying, private accounts
and energy allocation are installed laws. This does not demonstrate their
natural origin, self-maintaining organization, unassisted reproduction, evolving
heredity, adaptation or intelligence. Resource function here depends only on
the terminal bit; exact sequence inheritance is not evidence that every bit
has inherited useful function. All nutrients/atoms ultimately came from genesis.

Next science priority: separately precommit generic spontaneous assembly from

denominators. Check the added assembly/account-allocation law first; do not
convert this feasibility pass into a confirmation claim or retune TURNOVER-01.
