## The new question

Can ordinary mutation and paid inheritance produce organisms whose personally earned information improves useful actions, and whose blank-state descendants **reacquire** useful information in changed, reserved conditions with a fully paid reproductive benefit?

This is a new causal question for Ora, not a claim of global novelty in evolutionary computation. Repeating Pressure71's already-closed mutation/selection comparison with another label or larger bank would not answer it. Prior Learning81/Context90 showed scoped experience effects under supplied mechanisms, not naturally evolved learners; Inheritance83 did not establish beneficial hereditary selection. All old negative/partial results and the assistant's separate retention results remain unchanged.

The [machine-readable draft](../data/eka1-capability-proposal-20261023.json) specifies a functional conjunction, not an opcode/genome target:

1. An ordinary COPY mutation has authenticated paid ancestry.
2. Parent actions demonstrably use personally earned information under paid causal controls.
3. An actual paid, literal-faithful descendant begins with blank private state and reacquires under a reserved changed assignment.
4. The ancestral genotype does not explain that hereditary reacquisition effect.
5. The useful effect survives complete economic comparison against strong blind controls.
6. Independent origins and reserved conditions replicate the registered effect.

No novel-algorithm, broad intelligence, consciousness or acquired-answer inheritance claim follows automatically. An opcode, additional memory, a birth or a successful reflex is not enough. If an eventual assay addresses only one information channel, its claim must stay restricted to that channel; it must not label untested channels globally incapable.

Any genotype reversion, changed assignment, reference formation or lawful information intervention needs exact source and paid custody qualification. No authored reference enters discovery.

An indiscriminate state erasure can destroy motor phase or reproduction, while feedback corruption can change a fixed reflex. Neither alone proves loss of learned information. Exact lesion/sham and nonlethality controls remain **unimplemented and blocked**. No private target/presence oracle or free helper learner may resolve that gap. Single intact/sham/lesion forks do not supply within-World statistical replication.

Mandatory economic nulls are the predeclared balanced fixed-port allocation and the cheap blind/nursery-aware controls, with both fixed ports disclosed and all reference costs paid. The supplied feedback-reactive learner is a positive/efficiency benchmark. Requiring a naturally arisen learner to outperform that benchmark would test the stronger claim of superior efficiency; it is not required merely to show useful mutation-origin learning. No previous experiment's acceptance criteria are weakened by this distinction.
