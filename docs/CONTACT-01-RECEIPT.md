## What the negative means and next gate

This narrows ENERGY-02's positive possibility claim; it does not invalidate its
stable ordered authored result. There is no verified natural reproduction,
functional inheritance independent of supplied stock, learning or self-maintenance.
Food/input amounts, templates, initial work and supportive activation timing still
come from the experimenter. These deterministic arms are not ecological success
rates, independent evolving populations or measured chemical decay kinetics.

The loss obstruction is transparent accounting, not an unexplained emergence
failure: at most 3n locally stored units fund recognition n, ligation 2(n-1) and
child endowment 2, exactly 3n; release 1 comes from the parent. There is no activation
margin or renewal in the registered contact phase. Removing any needed precursor
potential makes this path unaffordable. Paid raw mismatches consume that margin.
The underlying activation rule can act again on an entirely empty ready precursor
if new food/work/contact opportunities exist; these paths schedule activation
only before the loss/contact rounds. Partial top-up is absent. Thus the protocol's
no-reactivation wording means no added or scheduled renewal mechanism, not a
proof that every other possible ordinary-activation schedule is impossible.
The registered all-precursor loss round is a strong controlled challenge, not
evidence about all weaker/temporally interleaved loss schedules or substrates.

Do not run longer founder-free worlds under a law missing their startup reaction,
or repeat this failed challenge with favorable retuned parameters. Next science
gate is prospective finite accounting for **generic resource-funded activation
renewal/contact scheduling and startup/assembly**, comparing existing empty-
precursor reactivation with any separately proposed partial-top-up rule, and
nonspecific with template-assisted paths
with supplied-founder/external-energy controls. No selected successful organism,
free work, new rewarded goal or covert reduction in old prices. A new physical
reaction requires a new precommitted study and independent controls before fresh
natural worlds. Reliable continuous execution cannot supply a missing reaction.
