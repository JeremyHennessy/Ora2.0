## Interpretation and limitations

The assay exercised paid local construction and access. Active-vs-ghost
conversion differs because the scientist installed that access gate; ghost
has physical carriers but no function. The fixed and external controls fully
reproduce the effect. No new productive semantics, autonomous constructor,
closed organization or self-maintaining individual was demonstrated.

Active received usable work from nutrient and consequently built109 carriers
versus ghost92, and had136 funded physical-carrier contacts versus ghost143.
These controls therefore do not share trajectory-total construction costs or
contact exposure even though each construction/read event has the same price
and proposals share a draw stream. No-assembly avoids construction costs and
is explicitly unmatched. Yoked equality is retrospective experimental source
matching, not an independent mechanism discovering the schedule.

Every work bank reached zero at64 ticks, including the active material-funded
case. Nutrient access alone did not sustain processing under these budgets.
Carriers persist passively at zero recurrent upkeep and can still decay after
work exhaustion. Construction stock is not recycled; maximum eight paid
births per funded history. Neither persistence nor occasional replacements
constitute endogenous repair. No transport, lineage, history controller,
environmental renewal or consciousness is tested.

## Decision and next concrete gate

Close this opportunity pilot as exercised; do not enlarge its horizon or tune
the bank, yields, decay or proposal rates to obtain persistence. Preserve its
fixed/table/external results as controls. No held-out confirmation of a
programmed access effect is scientifically justified.

Next specify a **physical constructor/production-network candidate** before
coding another science run. A system-produced, resource-accounted component
must be required for interface assembly, and its own production must have
logged material/work dependencies. Direct random construction from a shared
free-material bank is the null, not a constructor. Register constructor-
specific knockout, paid matched impairment and external constructor/source
comparators, all natural opportunity denominators and finite limits. This
tests a distinction beyond installed access rather than another successful
resource-capture count.

Before any damage/restart assay, the proposed law must expose paid production
of that constructor under an independently registered generator; avoid the
earlier sparse-recipe trap without selecting successful starting conditions.
The interface pilot's seeds and reserved8000..8063 remain unavailable for
tuning/confirmation. No automatic addition of genome, reproduction, memory
or protective entity. Evidence of paid regeneration would remain narrower
than life, heredity or indefinitely sustained organization.
