## Question and finite law

Can an explicitly paid recognition/binding intermediate and finite component
storage retain useful resource energy? Separate binding from conversion, price
savings from selectivity, physical storage from an unlimited account, and
component survival from externally protected work. No reproduction mechanism,
fitness reward, selected successful founder or observer-directed behavior.

Authored components exhaust all 28 binary sequences of lengths 2..4. Component
C owns n distinct body atoms, storing n-1 structural bond units. Every nutrient
atom has immutable bit and potential 8. All material starts externally supplied;
no ancestry/emergence claim follows from authored starts. Initial work budgets
0..n; work units have unique genesis IDs. Compare capacity n (one usable unit
per body atom, an explicit storage hypothesis) with an uncapped account null.
No capacity increase is allowed after seeing results.

Recognition costs up to 1 available work. With a live unbound component, an
available nutrient and a paid unit: complementary terminal bits bind; the
paid unit becomes binding potential 1. A nonmatching encounter dissipates that
unit to heat. Recognition while already bound, absent food/body or without
work cannot bind or charge another unit. This is installed affinity, not sensing
or learning. Binding protects no energy from subsequent component decay.

Processing requires a bound nutrient and 1 work. Matching food becomes waste,
returns three uniquely source/actor-linked work units; processing work plus the
binding unit plus five food units become heat (7). Overflow beyond capacity
goes to heat and is logged separately. Successful total debit remains 2, food
potential 8 and net uncapped work gain 1. A bound nonmatching nutrient instead
returns unchanged to the available stock; processing work/binding become heat.
Starved processing leaves bound food and binding potential unchanged. Release
returns bound food unchanged and dissipates its already-paid binding unit;
it supplies no free work. These finite transitions expose kinetic traps.

Decay moves all body atoms to waste, releases structural/binding potential to
heat, and returns any bound food unchanged. Local component work becomes heat;
a separately labeled protected-bank null retains its units/capacity after body
loss. Reclaim costs 1 work/1 heat and returns one waste atom as inert ready
material with recorded reclaimer/root. After body loss only the protected engine
bank can pay; that is external restoration, not endogenous reconstruction.
Waste food never regains potential. No body rebuild or copying action exists.

At every event conserve unique atom ownership, per-bit mass and total current
work + heat + structural bonds + binding potential + 8*(available/bound food).
Historical work-unit records are informational, not extra owners. Immutable
genesis roots, capture actor/nutrient/tick, disposal, overflow and reclaim records
must survive independent semantic replay. No unseen kinetic reservoir or free
recognition/filtering/regeneration is assumed.

## Controls and exhaustive cases

- Selective: paid complementary binding; mismatch costs 1 and does not bind.
- Nonspecific: paid binding of either bit, followed by paid processing/rejection.
- Price-yoked selective: rejected encounters additionally spend up to 1 remaining
  work to heat, matching the two-unit failed-encounter price of the old eager law.
- Capture ghost: same selective binding and processing debits, but matching food
  potential dissipates completely instead of returning work. Preserve consumption
  separately from useful energy capture.

Capacity n/uncapped and local/protected storage are orthogonal controls. Single
encounters/balanced paths use local storage only, because protection is inactive
there. Retention fixtures compare both storage locations and selective/ghost
returns, without redundant nonspecific/yoked matching-food branches.

Single encounter: 28 sequences, budgets0..n, both nutrient bits, four binding
controls and two capacities = 1,984 cases. Uniform recognize N0 then process.
Balanced path: 28 sequences, initial work2, foods[0,1,0,1], four controls/two
capacities = 224 cases. Uniform recognize/process/release for each food. These
are correlated authored schedules, not natural exposure or independent worlds.
Retention: all sequences/budgets, matching food, selective/ghost, both capacities
and storage locations, decay before or after processing = 1,984 cases. Before:
recognize/decay/process/release, then attempt reclaim of each body atom and food.
After: recognize/process/decay, then the same reclaim attempts. Total 4,192
cases; include all failed, starved, trapped and overflow states.

Independently enumerate uncapped, sufficiently funded complete-encounter drift
for matching fractions p=0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1: selective 2p-1, nonspecific/yoked 3p-2,
ghost -1-p. These algebraic expectations exclude storage saturation, starvation,
