# COUPLE-01: new-component construction, accounting feasibility only

Prospective registration, 2026-10-09. Preserve all closed REDOX, GRADIENT,
KINETIC and RECYCLE studies. This changes the causal substrate: individual
material atoms assemble into newly identified components through a separate
fuel pool, and damaged material must be reprocessed. It does not adjust any
old experiment's rates, resources, seeds or horizon.

## Question and interpretation boundary

Can a generic two-material catalytic resource-transformation network pay for
construction and complete material recycling/reconstruction from raw atoms?
Is reciprocal coupling necessary, or can independent components or an equally
efficient background channel do the same? Does finite stored fuel suffice when
the external photon reservoir is removed at damage?

This is exhaustive **existential reachability**, with a researcher-controlled
action scheduler. A witness is physical possibility under installed abstract
laws, NOT spontaneous organization, a natural frequency, self-maintenance,
reproduction, inheritance, learning or an organism. No witness or successful
network will be supplied as the founder of a future dynamics experiment.
Search depth/state counts are algorithm measurements, not physical time.

## Frozen accounting law

Four distinct raw atoms: A0,A1,B0,B1. No initial fuel, component or working
structure. Two raw atoms of material j manufacture one component Cj; these
atoms remain individually identified in its provenance. All components receive
fresh monotonically increasing object IDs. Material labels and the two-atom
construction rule are installed physics, not discovered sequences.

Energy is integer units. Initial photons P in {0,3,...,36}; no later inflow.
Free fuel Fj has potential one per unit; Cj has bound potential one; waste/raw
atoms have zero potential. Heat cannot be recaptured. Every reaction:

| Reaction | Inputs | Outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basal capture j | 3 photons | 1 Fj + 2 heat |
| Catalytic capture i->j | Ci unchanged, 3 photons | 2 Fj + 1 heat |
| Manufacture j | 2 raw j atoms, 3 Fj | new Cj (potential 1), 2 heat |
| Damage j | Cj | its 2 waste atoms, 1 heat |
| Recycle j | 2 waste j atoms, 1 Fj | the same 2 raw atoms, 1 heat |

Formation, product release and component endowment are included in manufacture's
3-unit price; there is no additional free activated precursor, release service
or fuel reset. Catalyst identity is retained, never consumed or copied by capture.
No kinetic/translation work is installed: a passive reachability oracle selects
legal reactions. This absence disqualifies any natural-dynamics/time claim.
Photons + fuel + component potentials + heat = initial P in every state.
Each material always has exactly two atoms across raw/component/waste forms.

All 16 directed 2x2 catalytic matrices are examined; bit 2*i+j means i->j.
No topology screening. Reciprocal means both cross edges are present. Pure
diagonal matrices are the independent-component controls. Topology is an
installed reaction relation, not inherited organization. Capture efficiency
is an explicitly programmed mechanism; its advantage is not a discovery.

## Fixed challenge and nulls

On the first state with both Cj present, damage BOTH immediately, regardless of
whether they were productive. All four atoms become waste. An endpoint is
existence of a later state with both fresh Cj present, all four atoms recycled
and no raw/waste atoms. Thus stored unconverted fresh material cannot substitute
for recycling. Component identities after damage must differ from before.
Damage is external intervention, not a spontaneous environmental event.

Five arms for each matrix and P, 1040 complete cases:

* `network`: the registered matrix and basal channel.
* `inert`: catalytic channel disabled; same material/photon endowment.
* `background`: unconditional 3-photon -> 2-fuel channel, same stoichiometry
  as catalysis, but no components required. An efficiency-matched null, not a
  claim about realized spatial encounter rates.
* `withdrawal`: network before damage; at damage all remaining photons become
  unavailable and are accounted as removed energy, never refunded. Afterwards
  only previously stored fuel can pay for reconstruction.
* `no-recycling`: network, but waste cannot return to raw atoms.

The initial raw inventory and P are identical across arms. Supplied founders
are excluded entirely; the oracle's authored action sequence is the assistance
that limits interpretation. Withdrawal removes photons, not scheduler assistance.
Post-withdrawal success cannot demonstrate activity after stored-fuel depletion.

## Success, falsifiers and stop decision

Accounting feasibility: at least one founder-free reachability witness under
`network` with exact energy, material, object identity and provenance accounting,
independently re-enumerated. Record complete counts, minimum P for each arm/matrix,
all infeasible cases and all explored states/edges. Compare reciprocal networks
to diagonal matrices, inert and background without significance tests: this is
an exhaustive finite possibility census, not random world samples.

No-recycling must have zero endpoints; any endpoint is a defect. No case with
less than 14 initial energy units can reconstruct: four manufactures consume
12 fuel, two recycles consume 2; capture loses energy, and damage refunds none.
Independent enumeration must match all cases, and witness interpreter must
reject payment, atom and identity forgery. Failure invalidates the implementation;
preserve it, investigate, prospectively refreeze before fresh execution.

If reciprocal topology is unnecessary or background performs at least as well,
do not claim organization-specific benefit. If no network is feasible by P=36,
stop this finite accounting candidate; do not increase P. If feasible, only a
separately frozen unselected local dynamics comparison with matched nulls could
test natural realization. Do not silently promote an oracle witness to a world.
