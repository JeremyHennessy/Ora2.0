## Registered endpoints and diagnoses

Primary: newly assembled post-128 product (size at least two), all construction
potential debits with both direct parents from actual capture, AND every component
having a latest prior returned cut that occurred post-128 and spent only actual
capture-origin work, AND a subsequent actual capture by that product. Components
may come from multiple cuts; no target identity, word or successful size is chosen.
This is capture-paid recycled functional construction, not reproduction, copying,
heredity, adaptation, self-maintenance or evolution. Supplied-arm success remains
assisted. Candidate success alone does not show autonomous origin: initial
external charging and preinstalled generic laws remain explicit assistance.

Separately report any assembly/probe; supplied-manufacture captures; post-bank-
withdrawal assembled captures; any/fully recycled releases; wholly capture-paid
fresh-material releases/probes; capture-paid recycled releases/probes; recycled
component counts, cuts/inert cuts and complete work/bond/charge heat costs.
Do not merge fresh-material, partial-reuse or externally paid recycling with the
primary endpoint. Future clean provenance is not imputed to contaminated history.

At each pre-step state, record unchanged RENEWAL-01 clean start/extension/release
and capture/food opportunities, plus large payers (size>=5), returned recycled
free atoms, post-128 affordable capture-paid cut pairs (chain size>=2), and such
pairs retaining at least two work afterward. Disable cut-pair opportunities for
irreversible and external modes, where the tested private cut cannot occur.
Classify actual paid cuts/charges/starts/extensions/releases/captures separately;
funding labels are observation only. Report opportunity sums, positive-state
counts and number of seed worlds with opportunities. Availability is not action,
and aggregate coincident opportunities are not complete trajectories.

Success requires at least two candidate seed worlds meeting the primary endpoint,
no matching endpoint in inert-cut/product-ghost/association-off/no-initial-work
nulls, and exact full audit/replay. A single candidate success is a preserved
isolated realization requiring fresh replication, not repeatability. Zero primary
success is a negative realization result in this bounded panel, not impossibility.
Food-withdrawn must have zero post-128 captures; inert controls must have zero
primary endpoint. Null violations falsify implementation/interpretation and must
be investigated before integration. Supplied/external alone cannot satisfy the
candidate claim. No inference of emergence rates beyond these finite samples.
