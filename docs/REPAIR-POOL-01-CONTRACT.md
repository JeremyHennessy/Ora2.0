# REPAIR-POOL-01: sharing must beat attainable local reserves

Prospective October10,2026. Exact accounting preflight for a distinct candidate
direction: sharing captured work to restore a component during a local source
interruption. This is NOT a complete mechanism or natural-world admission.
No prices are fitted, supplied apparatus declared autonomous, or closed laws
changed. Construction, material, transport dynamics and all-role renewal must
still be specified before this direction can become a mechanism.

## Smallest discriminating comparison

Four equivalent sites; one subsequently suffers a blind functional loss, with
all four losses included equally. Each arm has S already captured work units,
R units required for local replacement, and an identical finite future feed
whose restored useful output would be F=R+G. This prepared conditional output
is an optimistic possibility, NOT measured or a scripted successful trajectory.
Raw formation and apparatus renewal are not implied by paying R.

A stationary independent control allocates nonnegative integer reserves to the
four sites BEFORE loss, with total at most S. Enumerate every allocation. A site
can replace its lost component exactly when its reserve is at least R. It can
export all unused work: no control reserve is artificially stranded or discarded.
The best independent expected output, before common bills, is S+q*G where q is
the greatest equally weighted coverage of the four losses. An attainable witness
must accompany q. This is stronger than handicapping controls with uniform tiny
reserves. Independent controls are profitable whenever common bill C<S+q*G;
unknown C is not set to zero or claimed satisfied.

The candidate optimistic bound permits pooling after loss, provided S>=R+T,
where positive T is total additional work dissipated in transfer. Its whole-life
output bound before common bills is S+G-T-K. K is UNKNOWN, including every extra
formation/material/release/upkeep/replacement/load/waste charge of the pooling
apparatus. No unspecified role gets a zero bill. Thus the candidate can beat the
best independent only if K<(1-q)*G-T. Report that strict upper frontier and
the simultaneous profit condition C<S+G-T-K. If the frontier is nonpositive,
nonnegative apparatus costs cannot rescue it under this bound. If positive,
actual costs, endpoints and renewal may STILL rule it out. Positive frontier
is never admission. Unknown fixed physical/digital formation routes remain open.

Common C includes full raw-to-working startup/upkeep shared by both arms; specific
damage costs belong in R or K, including storage loss and builder replacement.
This model includes one prepared loss only, not whole-system repeated damage.
F requires fresh future feed and cannot reuse S. Source-exergy conversion costs
creating S and F must later enter C and the explicit source/waste ledger. The
mathematical bound deliberately relaxes that constraint; no full-cost surplus
is demonstrated. Viable independent allocation is a necessary comparator, not
a substitute for the eventual full-shuffle and function-disabled controls.
