2026-10-08. Precommitted measurement-only contract. No world sampling,

by this gate. These authored fixtures demonstrate auditor discrimination only.

## Rival substrates

Compare constructive process tokens with an immutable pair-to-product table.
Both pay identical costs. The latter can demonstrate causal replacement but
cannot support a claim that productive rules were constructed. Neither proves
functional equivalence, emergence, individuality, heredity or life.

## Frozen receipt law

Each unique token contains four integers in 0..3 and embodies four material
units. IDs are never reused. Initial tokens are investigator supplied.
`splice-v1` selects two distinct live parents a and b. A collision spends one
fuel unit, including failed collisions. If a[0] equals b[3] and at least four
precursor units remain, it must produce (a[1], b[1], a[2], b[2]), spending four
precursor units. Parents remain. Otherwise it produces nothing.
`fixed-graph-v1` instead uses a frozen ordered-pair table, with the same costs.
An unknown law is indeterminate and cannot yield a positive receipt.

Damage removes every live copy of the frozen target and prerequisite signatures
at one event; both must exist immediately before damage for eligibility. Decay
and damage transfer four units per token to waste. Explicit external rescue
spends four precursor units and one fuel unit. Rescue ancestry propagates to
every descendant and cannot count as internal restoration.

Every event records sequential numbering and the complete post-event ledger:
sorted live IDs, precursor, fuel and waste. The auditor independently rebuilds
these values and checks material conservation and finite fuel. Bounds are
1 MiB per input, 4096 events, 64 initial tokens and 128 simultaneous live tokens.
No executable payload, host I/O primitive or network instruction exists.

## Endpoint

Require one damage event, distinct frozen signatures, and terminal live copies
of both signatures born internally after damage. A rebuilt prerequisite must
participate directly as a parent in the birth of the terminal target. All
participating ancestry must be free of external rescue. This endpoint is paid
causal **payload restoration**, not restoration of demonstrated function or a
closed productive dependency set. Report eligibility separately, never remove
failures from denominators. Fixed-table positives remain comparator positives.

## Frozen authored fixtures and adversaries

Positive: initial A=(2,0,3,0), B=(0,1,1,2), C=(0,2,0,0),
old prerequisite P=(0,1,3,1), old target T=(1,2,3,0); precursor=20,
fuel=20. Remove old P/T, collide A/B to create new P, then new P/C
to create new T. Final precursor=12, fuel=18, waste=8; total material=40.
The identical fixed-table comparator maps A/B to P and P/C to T.

Required adversaries: no productive births, external prerequisite rescue,
dead parents, reused IDs, wrong products, omitted fuel/material costs, forged
state, incomplete damage, restored signatures without the required causal
parent, unknown laws, corrupted input digest and output overwrite attempts.
These are deliberately designed cases, not evolved or sampled evidence.
