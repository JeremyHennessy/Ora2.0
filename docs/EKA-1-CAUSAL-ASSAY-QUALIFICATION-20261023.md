## What the source establishes

Both pinned Pressure71 and SharedLineage68 implement the same relevant dispatch rules. Opcode 1 reads feedback; 2 reads a memory cell; 3 writes a memory cell from a register; 5 combines registers; 8 branches on a register; 9 writes code; 13 moves; 14/15 send and receive messages. Forage (6) overwrites feedback, including zero on an empty attempt. A recent feedback bit can therefore control a fixed reactive policy without demonstrating retention across a meaningful delay. Memory-cell use alone is neither necessary nor sufficient for learning.

Registers, private cells, code, control flow, position and social messages can carry history. Nursery progress, energy and ecology can also correlate with experience. These are candidate channels, not evidence that any ordinary organism uses them for learning. Clearing eight cells misses other channels. Clearing all state can break movement phase, copying, reproduction or survival. An apparent loss after erasure would not by itself identify acquired information.

The AST report lists direct `e[field]` syntax contexts only. A container marked Load may still be mutated through a nested index or alias (for example `reg` or `e['memory'][i]`). It is not a complete read/write dependency graph or dynamic taint analysis.

Both restore methods reconstruct a World from its original seed/configuration, replay every step to the requested tick, then assert exact snapshot hash equality. They verify historical continuation. They do not offer a separately authorized changed-state restoration operation. Deleting the equality assertion would remove custody validation rather than qualify a causal intervention. No such change was made.
