# TRANSMISSION-01 — ideal mechanical matching admission

Prospective finite accounting gate, October10. AI-only dimensionless virtual
mechanism, not physical hardware, biological engineering or empirical mechanics
calibration. No prior law, sample or controller changes. Supplied apparatus is
priced; no autonomous assembly or damage dynamics is implemented by this gate.

Hypothesis: a fixed-ratio five-unit gear train can repay complete declared bills
and exceed a three-unit lever with the same mechanical ratio on whole and
post-repair useful output. Unlike BUFFER capacity sharing, this mechanism
converts input rotation to usable load work by torque/displacement matching.

Constitutive ideal law: a pulse offers torque T through one angular unit, total
potential T. The external load has torque2. Mechanical ratio n means load travel
1/n and delivered torque n*T. A matched transmission can deliver w=2/n only
when n*T>=2; otherwise w=0. Conservation is T=w+unused potential; friction is
idealized zero identically for all arms, explicitly favorable to gears. No
torque amplification creates energy. No implemented motion/kinetics is claimed.

Four installed arms share twelve pulses and blind output-coupler loss after six:
gear n=2,N=5; stationary lever n=2,N=3; direct n=1,N=2; orientation-shuffled
gear n=1/2,N=5. The shuffle retains all five units and has attainable productive
output at T>=4. The lever is an explicit simpler primary alternative, not a
post-hoc omission. Ten material units in every arm; one coupler becomes scrap
and one virgin unit replaces it, leaving N live,1 scrap,9-N raw.

Every initial formed unit pays F, inclusive of transport/activation/formation;
every live unit pays m per pulse. Replacement pays R, inclusive of its entire
formation/transport/destruction bill. These declared virtual prices are not
empirical measurements or free construction. Unknown real/primitive rates and
costs remain uncalibrated. A generic fabrication possibility is assumed only
for a best-case accounting bound; actual accessible renewal is NOT established.

Register all T in{1,2,4,8}, F in{1/8,1/2}, m in{1/64,1/16}, R in{1/4,1}:
32 cases/128 arm ledgers, no seeds or worlds. Use optimistic gross output12*w
and post-output6*w, with no downtime and no externally funded repair credit.
Whole bound U=12*w-N*F-12*N*m-R; post bound V=6*w-6*N*m-R. Bills are charged
once. U,V are potential surplus bounds, not executed work stores or recovery
trajectories. Price omission or negative unused potential rejects the gate.

Before enumeration prove the same-ratio lever comparison algebraically:
U_lever-U_gear=2*F+24*m, V_lever-V_gear=12*m. A separate interpreter must
verify every exact row, material/exergy accounting and these differences.
Report all cases, all arms and opportunity counts, including nonpositive bounds.
Require an explicit positive whole/post bound in every primary control somewhere
before claiming even a prepared opportunity. Positive bounds alone establish
neither fabrication attainability nor self-maintenance.

If the lever strictly dominates on both endpoints throughout the registered

worlds or implementation of assembly/repair. This proof is conditional on the
declared equal-ratio ideal capture and uniform per-unit costs; it does not rule
out variable ratios, nonideal gearing, other laws or mechanical organization
generally. Do not add loss penalties to the lever to rescue the hypothesis.
