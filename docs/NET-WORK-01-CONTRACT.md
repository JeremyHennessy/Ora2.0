# NET-WORK-01 — admission contract for useful reconstruction

Prospective registration, October 9, 2026. This freezes the next question and
admission rules, not a new physical mechanism, parameter panel or fresh seed set.
No natural-world execution is authorized by this document alone. Preserve all
closed laws and their negative results.

## Question

Can organization convert finite environmental resources into independently
usable output, then reconstruct that conversion capability after blind damage,
leaving more usable work than its complete construction and maintenance cost?
Does it outperform equally funded independent and shuffled arrangements?

Growth, component counts, a restored shape, bond formation, continued chemical
activity and increased free energy are not substitutes for usable output.
Useful work must be a conserved, measurable increase in an explicit external
load or usable work store. The load must be available on identical terms to
all primary arms; its coupling is installed physics, not a discovered ability.

## Accounting and early stop

Before any fresh dynamics, freeze the physical law, all transition prices,
material and waste identities, reverse rates, resource accessibility, transport,
leakage, formation, release and repair costs. Count any imposed reservoirs,
boundaries, template/endowment, drive, geometry or actuation. Unpriced work,
one-way kinetic drive or an unlimited chemostat fails admission. Unknown costs
mean NOT ADMITTED, rather than zero cost or a proof of physical impossibility.

For a scoring interval define newly converted gross usable output G, all work
costs C, and other initial capital consumed D. Require surplus S=G-C-D>0.
Where costs are already debited from a work store, use the corresponding net
store/export change; do not subtract the same payment twice. Environmental
fuel is an explicitly priced finite resource, not an omitted initial reserve.
Thermal, chemical, structural and load reserves must have separate ledgers.
Retained capital must be checked at the interval boundaries. Charge destruction
and any operator intervention separately; it cannot be counted as harvested work.

Require BOTH positive post-damage surplus and positive whole-life surplus after
startup and every maintenance bill. An initial work reserve that pays repair
does not establish that subsequent conversion paid for repair.

Derive a conservative upper bound on G from accessible environmental exergy and
the exact law, and a lower bound on unavoidable costs. Stop before stochastic
simulation if even that bound gives S<=0, the endpoint is unreachable, or the
proposed advantage is excluded by a proved invariant. A loose positive bound
is only permission to continue feasibility analysis. Equal candidate/control
energy ceilings do not exclude a kinetic advantage: throughput, leakage,
retention and recovery probabilities can differ within the same ceiling.
Do not silently turn an efficiency question into a throughput claim.

## Equivalent primary opportunities

The independent and shuffled controls retain the same component inventory,
finite resource/work/thermal endowments, elementary conversion chemistry,
accessible load, material recovery, damage debit and observation horizon.
Changes in spatial access, opportunity frequency or channel proposals must be
priced and explicitly justified before freezing. A control forbidden to convert
resources or reach the endpoint is diagnostic only, not a primary comparator.
No control gets free material/endowment or a successful supplied organization.

The shuffle is fixed prospectively, with its own provenance; preserve components,
individual chemistry and total opportunities while disrupting the hypothesized
organization. Any ongoing imposed rearrangement must be paid and cannot become
an invisible external actuator. Demonstrate the same useful-work/repair endpoint
is physically attainable by both primary controls before sampling. If no fair
matched control can be specified, stop and redesign the question, not the result.

## Damage, reconstruction and acceptance

Freeze one blind damage schedule and target rule across arms. Score every
unscreened world; no retry or researcher rescue when useful organization has
not formed. Record actual pre-damage function and loss exposure. Reconstruction
requires fresh identified material/objects with paid ancestry and provenance,
followed by new environmental-resource conversion through the rebuilt capability.
Retained copies, reacquisition and unrelated background output are separate.

Freeze useful-output thresholds, whole-life/post-damage surplus, exposure gate,
finite horizon, depletion/withdrawal phase, paired comparisons, multiplicity
handling and stop criteria only after the exact mechanism passes admission.
Withdraw researcher-specific assistance while preserving matched natural
resource opportunities; quantify buffer depletion. Reserve independent samples
for reproduction. Any positive fixture remains controlled-only; acceptance of
self-maintenance requires independently reproduced unscreened repair and surplus.

## Immediate accounting review; no candidate dynamics

Review the exact accepted CONDENSE and RATCHET records read-only. Check the
CONDENSE work/fuel invariant for every world and calculate RATCHET net work
transfers separately from initial work capital and funded replacement counts.
Preserve source/data digests, formulas, outputs, exits and negative conclusions.
This is a retrospective accounting review under a prospective next-experiment
contract; it does not rescore their frozen endpoints or rerun their samples.

The active-droplet reference is not admitted as the primary NET-WORK experiment:
its inspected benchmark has imposed fuel drive and supplied droplet geometry,
without an explicit finite-fuel/load/repair-surplus ledger. Numerical reproduction
could inform a specific future physical law, but division alone cannot answer this
contract. A distinct converter/renewal law remains to be specified and audited;
do not invent prices or install an unproven transducer merely to get a positive.
