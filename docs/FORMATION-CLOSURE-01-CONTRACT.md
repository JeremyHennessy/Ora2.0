# FORMATION-CLOSURE-01: raw-start dependency preflight

Prospective October10,2026. A necessary-condition audit, not a new universe law,
mechanism, cost catalogue, natural-world panel or successful founder. Preserve
every closed experiment. The current scientific priority remains a complete
paid formation/conversion/repair mechanism; this audit cannot admit one.

Before spending resources on its dynamics, a proposed mechanism must name the
raw-to-functional transitions for every essential apparatus role, including
construction capability. Constitutive universe rules are not apparatus objects;
an installed constructor, field generator, store or converter is an object and
its formation prerequisites cannot be omitted. A source of environmental energy
does not by itself make any particular formation transition available.

## Exact finite question

Enumerate all512 three-role dependency catalogues. Each role has one formation
route with an AND prerequisite mask0..7. Empty prerequisites mean a declared
constitutive raw-start route, NOT zero formation cost or sufficient energy.
All material, space, energy and scheduling constraints are deliberately relaxed:
the calculation is an optimistic structural reachability bound only. Roles are
generic labels, not mappings to apparatus in an earlier experiment.

For each catalogue enumerate all8 initially working-role masks:4096 cases.
Mask0 is the raw-start question. Other masks diagnose supplied-apparatus rescue,
never seed a world. Every unavailable role can become available if all its
prerequisites are already available. Roles persist in this monotone abstraction;
finite inventories, work ancestry, damage identity and rates are NOT modeled.
Record each fixed-point closure and whether all3 roles become available. Also
compare each catalogue's zero-founder closure with a full loss of all working
roles: both have initial mask0, so any claimed differing closure is invalid.

The producer uses repeated set closure. A separate interpreter explores the
complete subset-state graph, one formation at a time, without importing the
producer. Match every case and deterministic replay. Reject missing, duplicate,
altered closure and energy-admission records. Authored tests cover cyclic
deadlock, a constitutive bootstrap chain, alternative seed masks and strict
input types. No stochastic samples, screening or post-result threshold tuning.

## Decision boundary

If full raw-start closure is absent even in this relaxed model, a mechanism
with exactly those prerequisites cannot assemble all roles from raw resources.
More energy or simulation time cannot create a missing route. This necessary
condition does not exclude a different explicitly specified formation route.
If closure is present, actual formation may STILL fail on finite energy,
materials, transport, exposure, rates, maintenance, damage or simpler controls.
Every output therefore has energy admission FALSE and costs UNKNOWN. Never
convert dependency reachability into a useful-work, repair or Stage3 claim.

This is not a comparison of viable physical mechanisms. The independently
profitable stationary/full-shuffle controls required for a future mechanism
remain to be defined and verified. No historical receipt is rescored.

Pin the reviewed producer, independent interpreter, tests and this contract
before execution. Existing operator lock;512MiB allocation,10CPU/10wall seconds,
4 processes;5GiB free-space floor. Evidence caps:raw8/archive8/restore8/failure1
MiB,25MiB total, enforced trusted prewrite accounting. Preserve process exits,
all cases, exact replay, failure evidence, hashes and restored-source re-audit.
No physical durability, full-host independence, pilot or observer publication.
