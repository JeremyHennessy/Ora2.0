# Independent original research conjectures — not claims of novelty or discoveries

**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Status:** original proposals, not independently validated; not a claim that no previous researcher has considered them. A complete prior-art search and ablation study are mandatory before making scientific priority/novelty claims. These are **new proposals for Ora2.0**, not derived from the prior Ora project.

Scientific antecedents: [Liu–Sumpter 2018](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA118.003795) reaction networks; [Hordijk/Steel autocatalysis algorithms 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11286130/); [Varanasi/Korenaga 2026](https://doi.org/10.1103/nmt5-qsym) catalytic core threshold; [Flow-Lenia 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569); [ToLSim 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01701). Links establish relevant research directions, **not support for the untested conjectures themselves**.

## N1 — Counterfactual organization fingerprint (COF)

**Question:** Can an evolving non-neural organization be identified by the **causal impact pattern of many localized interventions**, rather than by geometrical appearance, genome length or a hand-defined individual boundary?

**Proposal:** For a candidate structure, form a vector of counterfactual effects on resource throughput, constituent turnover and persistence after temporarily inhibiting each reaction, channel or local interaction. Compare this vector across growth, damage, fusion, splitting and partial replacement. An entity remains organizationally "the same" only if a predeclared similarity metric tracks **causal process contributions** through turnover.

**Competing explanations:** high-degree network graphs, reaction oscillators and passive attractors can also have stable intervention fingerprints; no proof of organismhood follows.

**Decisive test:** compare several blinded detectors on resource-dependent repair loops, passive conserved fields, inert oscillators and random graphs; require low false positives under held-out rules. Shuffle causal edge identity while preserving appearance. Pre-register similarity threshold on a separate dataset.

**Current relation:** AL01-DISCOVERY measures a **single component** of the proposed fingerprint. A full COF experiment is **not implemented**.

## N2 — Redundancy-versus-closure stress test (RVCS)

**Question:** Does apparent endogenous maintenance reflect distributed, replaceable repair organization—or merely one obvious reaction that the programmer can knock out?

**Proposal:** On a priori generated networks, intervene on 0/1/2 edges chosen by graph position before observing outcomes. Compare recovery profiles against degree/flux-matched random rewires, inert networks, and a topology where the only incoming reaction is deleted. A mechanism counts as **distributed** only if its recovery persists under partial deletion yet collapses under a targeted *cut set*, with resource-flow evidence.

**Falsifier:** recovery is entirely a function of total production rate, a single surviving incoming edge, basal rescue, or externally set resource supply.

**Why useful:** it explicitly guards against AL01-CAL's trivially repair-breaking positive control and against overinterpreting AL01-DISCOVERY's single cycle-edge ablations. This is a **proposed follow-up**, not an assumed result.

## N3 — Ecological memory without neural memory (EMNM)

**Question:** Could organisms or catalytic ensembles acquire useful **predictive traces** by modifying their world (waste, gradients, transient structures) without any designated neural architecture or memory register?

**Proposal:** Allow local reaction organizations to deposit a costly, decaying environmental trace that predicts future resource availability under patterned—but hidden—change. Natural viability and resource accounting supply any selection, **not a reward for prediction**. Compare trace-removal, cue-history shuffling, constant-resource and random-walk controls.

**Falsifier:** the supposed memory yields no out-of-distribution viability benefit once energetic costs, immediate resource levels and current sensory input are matched.

**Prior art warning:** environmental memory, stigmergy, biofilms, reservoir computing and extended phenotypes are established fields. Novelty, if any, lies in a rigorously stated combination and test design—not the idea of external memory.

## N4 — Organizational inheritance beyond sequence copying (OISC)

**Question:** Can heritable functional traits depend on **transmitted process organization** (network flux ratios / local reaction contexts) instead of an explicit digital genome or engine copy function?

**Proposal:** When a structure divides under local physics, track descendant reaction interactions and structural causes. Randomize daughter constituents while keeping boundary and feed equivalent; separately shuffle parental context or inherited rule parameters. Test whether descendants recover ancestor-specific functions on held-out perturbations.

**Falsifier:** apparent inheritance vanishes after controlling shared environment, or the only trait transfer is the simulator copying a fixed data structure.

**No premature architecture integration:** first establish ordinary lineage in a standalone instruction ecology. Do not bolt spatial chemistry to a genome until each substrate has independent measurements.

## N5 — Scarcity-induced loss of complexity (SILC)

**Question:** Under what conditions does higher organizational complexity **harm** persistence rather than promote intelligence, adaptation or life-like organization?

**Proposal:** Sample networks under resource budgets matched by actual delivered nutrient and update count; compare complexity (reaction count, causal cut-set size, and number of active functional components) with robustness across scarcity regimes, using predeclared nonlinear interactions rather than a single complexity score.

**Falsifier:** a model appears to benefit from "simple" structure merely because the simulator gives cheap simple arcs or penalizes extra edges explicitly; advantages vanish under matched physical costs.
