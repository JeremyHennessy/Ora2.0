## One next candidate card: COHERENT-CONTACT-01

This replaces the moving-contact proposal as the primary design question. It
is a genuinely different computational mechanism: collective mechanical
response to a finite oscillatory source, rather than sequential tape advance,
prediction or a supplied one-way clock. It is not an admitted mechanism, a
biological implementation, a successful founder or an activated controller.

### Resolved construction constraint: a pair-only conservative shortcut fails

This is a mathematical preflight for a precisely defined restricted model,
not a simulation or a claim about all possible formation physics. Let the
relative energy of two separated raw constituents be
`E_rel = K_rel + U(r)`, with `U(infinity)=0` and `K_rel>=0`. Define the required
stable functional pair as a state with `E_rel<0`. If their relative dynamics
are conservative and isolated, `E_rel` is constant. An incoming raw pair
therefore cannot reach that defined bound state. Passing through a low-potential
region temporarily is not stable formation: the lost potential becomes relative
kinetic energy rather than leaving the pair.

For target relative energy `-B`, where `B>0`, an incoming pair at relative energy
`E_in>=0` must transfer `E_in+B` to another represented degree of freedom, absent
other paid work transfers. This relation fixes a required ledger transfer, not
its rate or the numerical value of B. A finite third constituent/heat recipient,
an emitted-energy channel, or explicit source work extraction could provide it.
Its energy, material/state ancestry, available capacity and reverse channel must
be represented. External extraction is assistance until its apparatus is formed
and renewed; an unexplained damping term cannot count as that apparatus.

Consequently, reject a two-body conservative or permanently installed-spring
shortcut as this candidate's raw-formation implementation. The next explicit
missing item is a raw-accessible association/energy-release channel with a finite
recipient and an executable reverse path. This prerequisite does not establish
functional source-to-load conversion, a profitable control or all-role renewal.
No B, rate, efficient capture probability, price or favorable sample is assigned.

**Question.** Can a structure formed and reformed from raw constituents deliver
more retained mechanical work from the same finite oscillatory source than
attainable simpler, profitable converters, after its entire construction and
renewal bill?

**Distinctive causal capability.** A coupled structure might change mechanical
source-to-load impedance and retain useful transfer after its links are renewed.
That possible benefit is a hypothesis. A resonance peak or absorbed energy is
not retained useful work and does not establish repair.

**Exact proposed comparison.** Candidate: two raw-formed capture subassemblies
coupled to a shared output link. Simple implementations: two independently
loaded subassemblies on the identical source, and the cheapest accessible
single passive converter using the same total material. A stationary design
with the same necessary coupling is also eligible; it cannot be excluded by
calling it unorganized. Apparatus histories must make these roles concrete
before any comparator is claimed implemented or profitable.

**Smallest decisive test.** One finite source-to-load cycle starting from raw
material, then a predeclared functional loss and fresh source opportunity.
Produce actual formation, source-work, load-work, release and renewal histories
for candidate and both viable simple implementations. No frequency sweep,
parameter census, seed search or natural-world panel is authorized by this card.
If the first complete cycle is not feasible, reject before further exposure.

**Complete bill.** Finite source preparation and delivery/exergy; every raw
identity and stored constituent potential; formation and release of every
capture, coupling, confinement and output role; construction capability where
it is an apparatus; upkeep, friction/heat, transport, initialization and any
state/memory; work delivery and retention; functional damage, material recovery
and new component identities. Any imposed source timing, clamp, bath, damping,
load latch or scheduling support must be identified and paid within its proper
source/environment/apparatus boundary. Calling it a field or substrate is not
evidence that it is constitutive physics. Unknown terms remain UNKNOWN.

**Named missing law and concrete resolution.** A constituent-level
formation/coupling/load law with the finite association-energy recipient above
is absent. First specify raw, bound and released
states and a single energy function covering source, material, all apparatus
and the work reservoir. Derive each force/transition debit from energy changes;
include both directions and finite heat/waste storage. Show an accessible
raw-to-bound formation path and a paid output transfer that remains available
after coupling has ended. Then remove every essential role and replay its
raw-to-functional renewal with new identities. An oscillator response equation,
arbitrary assembly fee or a damping term labeled entirely as useful output
cannot fill this gap. If those paths cannot be specified, reject this candidate;
do not silently install springs, clamps, latches or a constructor.

**Gates.** All-role formation/renewal and finite ancestry first; then positive
whole-life and fresh post-loss useful surplus, with profitable simpler controls
and a strict candidate advantage. Keep incoming source/resource identities
fixed under full internal-coupling/history shuffle; charge actual interventions.
Function-disabled diagnostics retain apparatus costs. Independently interpret
and replicate before accepting self-maintenance. No provisional numerical price,
force coefficient, source frequency or successful configuration is chosen here.
