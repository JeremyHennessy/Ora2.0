## Designs compared and decision scope

Interface candidate: a locally constructed four-material carrier enables
conversion of otherwise inaccessible nutrient at its site. Trace candidate:
a four-material marker is constructed after nutrient consumption and a fixed
local readout suppresses later processing at that site. Neither uses a global
visited array, target score, host code execution, organism object or LLM.
Both laws/readouts are authored physics. A finite table/reflex implementation
of each is an explicit strongest null and should match active event histories.
Do not rank their raw yields head-to-head: they perform different tasks in
different authored contact sequences. This gate checks costs and observability.

Use only deterministic authored two-site cases; no random genesis or organism
seeds. There are seven cases per candidate, four shared arms (active, ghost,
fixed, no_field), and an additional external_flux interface arm:63 receipts.
These are fixtures, not63 independent experimental worlds. No natural-exposure
probability can be inferred. A later pilot requires its own precommitted
generator, local encounter proposals and finite opportunities before execution.

## Common finite ledger

Two sites, each at most one carrier. Start with eight free material units,
12 usable-work units, no carriers/waste/heat unless the case overrides a budget.
Each nutrient unit has one material and eight abstract potential units.
Nutrient may be at a site or in a finite reserve escrow. No replenishing input.
Conserve free material +4 per carrier +nutrient +waste. Conserve usable work
+8 per nutrient +heat. These abstract accounts are not thermodynamic proof.

Construction consumes four free material and two work -> carrier +two heat;
ghost constructs a physically present nonfunctional carrier with identical cost.
no_field suppresses construction and its cost; it is explicitly an unmatched
cost baseline. Interface construction is an authored build proposal; trace
construction is attempted automatically after each successful conversion when
the local slot is vacant. Neither may write without material/work or overwrite
an occupied carrier. Record every unavailable proposal, including budgets.

Contact costs one work ->heat for local reading. If no work remains, record
read_unaffordable and do nothing. Trace active/fixed skips further processing
when a functional marker is present (ghost/no_field do not). All other contacts
attempt processing at a further one work ->heat. If unaffordable, record it.
Interface active/fixed may convert only when its functional carrier exists;
ghost/no_field cannot. External_flux builds the same paid ghost, then converts
when that ghost is present, recording conversion source external. This is an
authored matched-source comparator, not autonomous capture or a yoking protocol
for future diverging trajectories. Trace conversion needs no interface.

Successful conversion moves one nutrient material to waste and its eight
potential to three usable work +five heat. Thus contact net gain is one work
before construction costs. No work, material or usable source is created free.
Record successful/failed reads, processing, conversion and construction in order.

renew moves at most one nutrient from reserve to a selected site, without
creation. withdraw moves all site nutrient to reserve. These are tagged
external environmental interventions, not organism action. erase consumes
one work ->heat and converts the local carrier's four material to waste;
it never returns free construction material. foreign attempts the same paid
carrier construction, tagged external; it is not evidence of endogenous history.

## Gates and next experiment

Any negative budget, free work/material, occupied-slot overwrite, external
carrier mislabeled internal, ledger residual or fixed-null divergence blocks
admission of either kernel. Retain the failure rather than changing cases.
Trace renewal and foreign-marker cases must expose suppressed accessible
resource use; do not call longer state retention beneficial by definition.

If the ledger/control calibration passes, prefer an interface-first **design**
for the next finite pilot because local conversion can be measured without
adding a history controller. Trace remains a competing future mechanism whose
fixed readout and harm under resource changes must be separated from learned
memory. This choice is assay simplicity, not demonstrated superiority.
Before any pilot, register natural local build/contact opportunity counts,
controls for disabled assembly/function and externally matched source flux,
generator and finite budgets; inspect no new random seeds in this calibration.
Neither kernel passes a repair, heredity, utility-generalization or life gate.
