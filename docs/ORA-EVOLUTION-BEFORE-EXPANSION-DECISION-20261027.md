## 1. North star and the claim we actually want

Our primary research goal is **one naturally arising organism-native, information-dependent capability that is causally beneficial after every real cost, is reachable under ordinary variation, and improves lawful descendant performance**. Build outward only after each part is separately established.

We prefer one modest independently replicated advantage to a larger simulation, a clever supplied controller, more PRs, more host assistant epochs, or visually convincing playback. No unqualified claim of general intelligence, open-ended evolution, sentience, selfhood, or scientific novelty.

**Do not confuse these six achievements:**
1. An authored VM permits feedback and memory.
2. A naturally produced organism executes an information opcode.
3. An organism's *own* informative observation changes a later decision.
4. That decision has positive net resource/fitness value against strong cheap rules.
5. Ordinary mutation and paid inheritance produce and preserve the beneficial capability.
6. Independent cohorts reproduce the result with registered unseen conditions.

Each receives a separate status. One does not imply the next. Host Ollama memory, Context90's scoped retrieval, finite PILOT74 renewal, and historical/schematic Observer animations are not organism intelligence.

## 3. The scientific bet: make *information worth paying for*

**Working hypothesis:** In an ecology where the same individual can encounter predictively useful information before a later choice, a *small, naturally attainable* rule governing whether/when to retain its own observations could outperform efficient fixed/reactive competitors, after sensing, memory, decision, failed-action, copying and reproductive costs.

This is a **hypothesis**, not a selection of a winning genome or permission to create a new ecology. First test the *opportunity* rather than designing a learner to fit an unqualified task.

**Identifiability gate (first science question):**
- Under the unchanged native source laws, find or rule out two legally reachable histories with identical *currently available* actor inputs but different future best actions, where a **personally earned earlier observation** distinguishes them. Include how often the opportunity can occur before death/expiry and whether resources exist to act on it.
- Include matched nonpredictive cue and no-information cases. A benefit that survives when the cue contains no predictive information is **not** evidence for the proposed learning mechanism.
- Compare the actual observable policy class, source timing, packet presence, earlier attempted port, location/phase, scheduler and actor lifetime. Different-site observations are not automatically useless; a source identity that is only available to the analyst is not a legal organism signal.
- Start with bounded source/closed-receipt analysis already legitimately accessible. Do **not** reread old seed banks, manufacture a historical selected winner, run a VM/World, or post raw/private local evidence. If existing data cannot decide, say UNKNOWN and state the minimum prospective authorized observation needed.

**Affordability gate (second question):**
- Before selecting a complex policy, account for the founder's *cash flow before first reward*, not just eventual mean accuracy: formation/material, sensing, reads/writes, updates, failed attempts, time/expiry, caps, transfers and minimum COPY/division costs.
- Compare legal efficient fixed0/fixed1, alternator, last-feedback/reactive and compact finite-state references where each is applicable. Do not assume a larger q8 memory or a supplied neural learner is the right reference.
- The target is a reproducible positive **net within-life advantage** on prospectively reserved/held-out instances; this is a prerequisite for spending on an evolutionary population, **not** a claim that isolated learning proves evolution. Include true/sham, nonpredictive-cue, erased-history and cost-matched no-information comparisons. Do not treat XOR corruption as a no-sensor null or per-World optimal oracle.
- Do not lower prices, raise reward, extend life, or increase founder subsidy *after* seeing a negative. A different ecology/substrate, if necessary, must be a versioned alternative proposal with new independent hypotheses and a matched unchanged-law baseline.

**Reachability and inheritance gate (third question):**
- Identify legal mutational origins and viable intermediate paths under existing native code/ancestor and actual mutation distribution; count the real opportunities for expression, information encounter, copying, birth and descendant action. A crafted program or subcube gives a conditional bound, not an evolved origin.
- Preserve whole-population and shared-seed/block denominators, including non-readers, deaths, unexpressed mutations and nonbirths. An inherited READ opcode is weaker than inherited paid learning capacity; inherited private answers/culture are different hypotheses.
- Require causal genotype/experience interventions and unseen conditions before any statement that selection favored an evolved learning routine. Do not seed a winner or retrospectively promote an endpoint.

## 4. Gates and branching decisions

| Gate | Pass / candidate GO | Candidate NO-GO | UNKNOWN / blocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| O: informative lifetime opportunity | Source-backed actor-visible predictive contrast reachable before expiry | Legally available inputs make past information redundant for the **specified candidate task** | Timing/encounter data or authority missing |
| A: affordable within-life mechanism | Prospectively tested net benefit exceeds strong cost-matched references in held-out conditions | Predeclared test fails, or honest benefit upper bound cannot pay mandatory costs | Unmeasured costs or source-bound eligibility |
| M: mutational and hereditary corridor | Lawful ordinary origin, viable expression, paid reproduction and descendant contribution are supported | Source law rules out the **specified corridor** | Path/supply/offspring evidence insufficient |
| N: native custody and safety | Independent exact original-owner/source/config/raw/compiled-code/closure/resource qualification, safe CI and separate admission | A specific proposed qualification is rejected with preserved evidence | Original event not created, original-owner/fixtures/limits missing |
| E: full evolutionary claim | Independently registered, complete-population natural origins plus causal fully paid inherited advantage and replication | Registered evolution/fitness endpoint fails | Never infer from O/A/M/N alone |

**Stop rule:** Fail a predeclared candidate honestly and preserve it. Do not run the same candidate with newly favorable settings. If O or A is decisively NO-GO for the chosen candidate, close *that* mechanism and compare one small independently versioned alternative without modifying the original. If the substrate itself remains UNKNOWN, do not label it impossible.

**Pivot rule:** Only after source-backed evidence identifies a substrate-specific barrier should we compare an explicitly versioned **minimal alteration to that barrier**, with all new priors, prices, comparator, power, and approval declared before execution. No architecture replacement on the present noisy-credit negative alone.

No arbitrary percent-complete intelligence score and no fabricated timelines. The milestones are evidence gates, not schedules.

## 6. Codex iteration discipline: fewer changes, stronger results

For every future authorized Codex iteration:

1. **Reconstruct** current main SHAs, PR heads, worktree/owner status, actual checks and dated native evidence. Never infer local Windows state from GitHub.
2. **Choose one highest-information, dependency-safe question** that existing owners are not already answering. Prefer the smallest source-only/cost/identifiability proof over speculative architecture.
3. **Write the hypothesis and falsifier before changes.** Separate immutable observed facts, assumptions, conditional deductions and missing measurements.
4. **Make one reversible, minimal intervention** in an independently owned branch or the current authorized owner branch. No cleanup/refactors during a fix, no modifications to approved science or UI, and no silent changes to prices/selection.
5. **Validate exactly what changed.** Tests, independent source pin/calculation checks, actual PR head/CI, native custody and screenshot QA where appropriate. Never call a skipped or failing broad workflow PASS; if CI could rerun frozen scientific work, do not invoke it.
6. **Record both success and failure** with complete denominators, raw provenance boundaries and authentic role/owner IDs kept private. Do not conflate synthetics, source tests, experimental originals, learned behavior, evolution and culture.
7. **Close, hold or pivot.** No new PR without a materially new independent deliverable. No artificial commits/comments, duplicate custodians, or speculative chains. If the answer is UNKNOWN, name one next missing proof, not a broad task list.
8. **Report:** kid-friendly account and a technical table: baseline/current SHAs, branch/PR, tests run/omitted, exact source links, scientific verdict, blockers, single next action, and whether anything was merged, deployed or live.
