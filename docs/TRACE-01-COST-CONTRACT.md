# TRACE-01 — passive experience-state resource gate, before behavior

Prospective component experiment, not a controller/agent/world or a learning claim.
LAUNCH-01 and all closed science laws remain immutable. This gate implements no
action selection, successful policy, reward, neural/LLM system or legacy Ora change.
Author-supplied component geometry and pulse trains are feasibility fixtures only.

Question: can an explicitly priced physical trace retain, lose and reconstruct
state with finite fuel, material and operator input, and what retention horizon
already makes useful readout impossible before any behavioral test?

## Fixed passive law and prices

Sixteen identical sites, four identified structural atoms/site(64 total), one
metastable potential/pin at each site, initially unactivated. The deliberately
supplied chamber/site geometry is installed test hardware, not autonomous origin.
Each arm receives256 individually identified fuel units and64 operator-work units;
gross320. No regeneration/refund or new matter; output never earns resources.
All endowment, inputs, activated potential and heat remain in the full ledger.

* Write attempt: require two fuel and one external pulse unit. Pulse becomes heat.
  At a free site, candidate activates one pin potential plus one heat from the two
  fuel. At an already activated site, both fuel become heat. No favorable fallback.
* Read: one fuel -> one heat; returns installed local trace occupancy only. The
  output is measured, not sent to an action-selector. No meaning/correct answer
  supplied to the component. Constant reactive readout is installed control physics.
* Retention clock: one fuel -> one heat per chamber clock step in EVERY arm,
  including disabled/fixed-state controls. This is a declared finite refresh price.
  Once unaffordable, every active pin potential dissipates into heat and resets;
  no fuel refund. A later unaffordable read returns null, not a zero observation.
* Erase: active pin potential -> one heat, same material retained at its site.
* Reconstruct: another normally priced write, new trace identity and parent fuel/
  pulse provenance. Reconstruction of this installed component is not reproduction.

Finite local chamber availability is an explicit feasibility assumption. Future
world delivery, sensing, movement, trace-address wiring and useful interaction
costs must be separately accounted; this gate proves none of those functions.
