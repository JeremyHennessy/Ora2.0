# TEMPLATE-01 — paid copying and resource-coupling feasibility

2026-10-08. Freeze before implementation or execution. Based on Ora main
c0d20b02 and AI-Research 43d6abfb/Pass 20. TURNOVER-01 remains closed.

## Question and limits

Can an explicitly installed, sequence-general template reaction conserve energy
and unique material while a component's own nutrient interaction finances
production of a functionally matching descendant? This is a feasibility test,
not a neutral opportunity panel or a demonstration of spontaneous reproduction,
selection, adaptation, inherited organization or autonomy. No fresh scientific
seeds or reserved samples will be used. All 28 binary templates of lengths 2..4
are equally tested as authored starting objects; none is a selected organism.

## Frozen physical law

Atoms have immutable IDs, bits and external genesis provenance. All atoms are
in one object, unused nutrient, ready monomer or waste. Objects retain atom
order, a unique ID, template-parent ID, birth event and private work account.
Object history survives decay. Heat is global and unusable. Each polymer bond
stores one potential unit; nutrients have potential 8. No input is replenished.

Contact costs 2 work from the contacting object's account (1 read/1 process).
Length >=2 and terminal-bit complement enables nutrient conversion to waste,
returning 3 work to that same account and 5 additional heat. Net +1 work/+7 heat.
If only 1 work remains, read costs 1 and conversion cannot occur. Missing or
nonbinding nutrients still incur available contact costs. No free start-up.

Copying retains a template and consumes n distinct ready monomers matching its
ordered bits. Recognition costs n, ligation costs 2(n-1), release costs 1:
physical production charge 3n-1, stored bonds n-1, heat 2n. Additionally transfer
2 work from parent to new child as explicit start-up endowment: total debit
3n+1. Recognition and product construction are installed mechanisms, not a
discovered copying ability. No desired sequence, reward or extra energy.

Paid ghost: when template copy is feasible, debit the same 3n+1 entirely to heat,
without product or consumed monomers. Un templated priced null: same charge and
endowment, but takes the first n ready atom IDs without checking template bits;
its product has no template parent. Shared-bank control: same template relation
and price, but contact/copy charge and return use one global work bank; new
objects have zero private work. It demonstrates a subsidy route separately.

Decay converts an object's atoms to waste and bonds to heat; residual private
work becomes heat. A surviving object can pay 1 work/1 heat to regenerate one
waste atom into a ready monomer. Neither copying nor regeneration duplicates
atoms, converts heat back to work, or creates fresh nutrients. Total per-bit
mass and work+heat+bonds+8*unused nutrients must remain constant after every step.

## Authored finite tests and falsifiers

For each of 28 sequences, provide a template with exactly 2 work, one finite
ready reserve (both bits, sufficient for two copies), and finite nutrients.
Active schedule: 3n-1 complementary nutrient contacts by the parent, one copy,
3n-1 contacts by its child, one further copy. Each child is funded only by the
declared endowment and its own conversions. Include all charged recognition,
ligation, release and endowment; report remaining feedstock and total energy.
Use identical proposed schedules/reserves in ghost, un templated and shared
controls, and report lineage/function differences rather than requiring all
nulls to fail. A matching null product is not template-specific inheritance.

Separate all-sequence fixtures test zero/partial work, insufficient/wrong ready
feedstock, nonmatching nutrients, paid decay/reclaim and a repeated copy using
regenerated material. These are intervention fixtures, not independent worlds.
Exhaustively check binary sequences 2..4 and budgets at copy debit -1/debit/
debit+1; static ledgers and actual executor results must agree with an independent
auditor that imports no worker. Reject material/energy, parent and cost tampering.

Feasibility passes only if every active sequence yields two paid, atom-distinct,
sequence-matching generations with funded child contact, conserved ledgers and
correct provenance, and controls/negative cases obey the law. Otherwise preserve
failure and close this law without adjusting prices or selecting sequences.
Even passing feasibility does not admit a confirmation panel: separately
precommit neutral formation, encounter exposure and stronger causal controls
before any fresh worlds. Private work association is installed bookkeeping,
not a discovered membrane or selective ecological organization.

Pass 20: distinguish material identity, source acquisition, parent-linked sequence
production, useful descendant function and repeated reproduction. All 28
starting templates count, including failures; do not count only successful
offspring or confuse reserve consumption with sustained external autonomy.
