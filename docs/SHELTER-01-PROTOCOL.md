# SHELTER-01 — shared protection opportunity gate

Prospective October 10, AI-only dimensionless universe. A new geometric
damage-interception law, not a change to any frozen experiment. This gate tests
necessary accounting opportunity only. It cannot establish autonomous maintenance.

Nine converters each accept one environmental packet of potential4 per tick,
deliver useful work2 and dissipate heat2. There are72 ticks in three24-tick
windows. Both converters and passive armor intercept a damaging ray at its
first occupied cell; interception destroys that unit. Scrap is inactive and
retained. Armor is transparent to the separately declared packet channel.
This selectivity is installed toy physics, not a demonstrated real material.

At each window boundary24,48, supply36 rays: one from each cardinal direction
aimed at each converter's location. Every ray starts outside the arrangement.
After the rays, independently remove converter identity0 in every arm, even
if protection is intact. Thus both essential material roles incur replacement
bills; protection-only turnover cannot masquerade as restored converter function.
For an optimistic necessary bound, replace each loss immediately before the
next ray. No downtime is charged because this is explicitly an upper bound,
not measured kinetics. No assembly/recovery trajectory or ancestry is claimed.

Five stationary prepared arms: shared shelter (nine converters in a3x3 core,
sixteen armor cells forming its5x5 perimeter); independent nine separated bare
converters; compact bare3x3 converters that can shield one another; individually
protected nine converters each with eight neighboring armor cells; uniform full
shuffle of the nine converter/sixteen armor labels across all25 cells. Rays
and packet opportunities per converter are identical. Work pooling is optimistically
available to every arm as an accounting relaxation, not a free bus implementation.

All live cells are opaque to rays, including converters: compact self-shielding
is explicitly included. Nevertheless every bare-arm ray destroys one converter.
The individual and shared shelters lose one armor cell per ray. In the shuffle,
condition on an aimed converter at a uniform cell:4/5 of directions have a
distinct boundary cell; that cell is armor with probability16/24. Hence the
exact expectation is8/15 armor hits,7/15 converter hits per ray. Uniform shuffle
includes the working arrangement and all label configurations. Expected fractional
counts are ensemble moments, never fractional material identities or sampled worlds.

Every raw-to-converter unit pays W in{2,4}; every raw-to-armor unit pays A
in{1/4,1}; converter upkeep p in{1/32,1/8}, armor upkeep q in{1/64,1/16} per
tick. Formation prices include activation, placement and transport. Every
replacement pays the same role formation price plus release/removal D=1/4.
Initial construction, ongoing maintenance, both damage bursts and both converter
losses are charged. There are192 material units in every arm;74 replacements
leave74 scrap, live9/25/81 and raw192-live-74. No recycling credit or refills.
Unknown primitive rates, autonomous placement, selective permeability and actual
placement cost are NOT calibrated by these declared prices; not silently zero.

Register all16 price cases/five arms:80 ledgers, no seeds or worlds. Whole bound
is gross1296 minus formation minus72-tick upkeep minus both repair bursts.
Post bound covers the48 ticks after the first loss: gross864 minus48-tick
upkeep minus both repair bursts. Report first-window bank after formation/upkeep
and first burst to check prospective repair fundability, not just terminal surplus.
Environmental potential2592 = useful work1296 + conversion heat1296. All bills
consume useful work and dissipate heat; whole accounting must include these bills
without counting the conversion heat as work. Supplied construction credit is
fully repaid in the whole bound and is not endogenous startup evidence.

Require positive whole/post bounds and nonnegative first repair-bank bounds for
EVERY primary control in EVERY registered case, and a strict shared-shelter
advantage on both endpoints over EVERY comparator, including compact self-shielding
and the full-shuffle ensemble mean. A separate rational interpreter must derive
the shuffle expectation using cell/direction counts, verify all80 ledgers and
reject unpaid repair/formation or energy corruption. If this gate passes, only
complete raw-to-role formation and finite damage dynamics design is justified.
Do not launch natural worlds until an independently reviewed complete law shows
accessible formation/renewal, useful loss exposure, fresh output and viable controls.
Expected bounds are not paired sample statistics or repeatable self-maintenance.
