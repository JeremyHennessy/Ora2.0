# BUFFER-01 — finite capacity sharing and useful contact reconstruction

Prospective accounting admission, October 10. This is a new virtual-universe
candidate, not a change to RETENTION, CONDUCTION, RECTIFY or any prior law.
It tests temporal capacity sharing, rather than binding or diode conversion.
No naturally formed apparatus, physical hardware, adaptive controller or fresh
stochastic worlds are claimed or activated. No biological mechanism is involved.

## Falsifiable question and frozen finite panel

Can two converters sharing two units of finite work capacity deliver more useful
work after a paid contact replacement than attainable independent stores, while
also repaying all startup costs? A preserved useful buffer is insufficient:
fresh work from the replaced contact must reach the external load.

Enumerate all sixteen four-bit source patterns, repeated four times after one
initial tick exposing both sources. Seventeen ticks, with blind loss of contact
0 or 1 after tick 8. The initial both-source tick is environmental exergy charged
on identical terms in every arm, not a work endowment or a selected founder.
Both source labels and every pattern remain in the denominator. Resource packets
expire as opportunities but unconsumed potential remains in the fuel ledger.

Freeze bus formation price in {1/2,1}, upkeep per formed material unit per tick
in {1/32,1/8}. Total128 cases, five arms each: connected candidate; two independent
unit stores; independent double-capacity store at source0; the same at source1;
and permuted bus endpoints forming a self-loop instead of a cross-store link.
The self-loop retains both independently accessible converters/stores, complete
inventory and candidate prices/upkeep. It can reach the endpoint and is not
forbidden to capture, construct or deliver output.

The stationary independent envelope is the best of all three independent
allocations separately for whole-life and post-damage output, with that
post-hoc advantage disclosed. Single-source controls pay only for their formed
converter and can leave the other site unbuilt; blind damage to that vacant
site causes no loss. All arms retain the same ten raw material units, external
load and offered resource tape. This deliberately favors simpler alternatives.

## Exact virtual law and full accounting

Each available packet has four units of potential. A functional collector/contact
pair converts it to two work units and two heat units; overflow becomes heat.
All stores begin empty. A collector without its contact consumes no packet.
Common connected capacity is2; independent allocation is(1,1), (2,0) or(0,2).
The loop has(1,1). The identical external load accepts at most1/2 per tick,
through an installed passive cutoff: total buffer work below1 is not exported.
No sensing, learned policy or privileged source forecast is provided.

Work is held with provenance (before loss, surviving post-loss, rebuilt contact).
Debits from separate stores are proportional to stored work, including upkeep,
construction and load. This favorable common withdrawal access is explicitly
available to independent arms too; they cannot silently obtain less useful loads
or repair opportunities. Connected capacity changes capture/spillage only.

Every formed component consumes one raw material unit and1/4 work for transport.
Formation work: collector1, contact1/2, each capacity unit1/2, constructor1/2,
external load1/2, cross-link/self-loop the frozen bus price. A double-capacity
store still consumes two capacity units and pays both formation/transport/upkeep
costs. Single-source arms form six units; balanced independent eight; candidate
and loop nine. Raw inventory is10, with unformed units retained.

Startup formation/transport is supplied, charged in full, then withdrawn:
the candidate apparatus is installed, not autonomously originated. Unused
startup allowance is returned before harvesting and never enters a buffer.
No environmental resource or work is replenished during the certificate.

Each tick: offer packets; charge capture and heat; pay upkeep for every currently
formed unit; attempt missing-contact construction if a virgin raw unit exists
and remaining buffer work covers3/4 (transport1/4 plus formation1/2); export to
the load above the cutoff. These installed fabrication rules are a prepared
capability, not evidence of naturally established construction machinery.
Failure to pay upkeep consumes the available work as heat and stops that arm;
preserve its inventory, unused fuel, exits and failed endpoint. No rescue/retry.
Loss converts exactly one formed contact to scrap and removes its function;
the collector and store survive. A replacement has a fresh identity, uses virgin
material and must capture fresh resource and contribute fresh load output.
No recycling credit is granted. All setup, transport, formation, upkeep,
conversion, overflow and failed-upkeep work is heat; none is usable output.

Require the exact energy identity:
startup allowance + consumed environmental potential = returned allowance +
startup heat + conversion/overflow heat + upkeep heat + replacement heat +
external-load work + final buffer work.
Also require offered potential = consumed + unconsumed, and10 material units =
raw + live + scrap, including surviving collector and replaced-contact identity.

Whole surplus is load work + final buffer - startup cost. Post surplus is load
work since damage + final buffer - buffer at damage; maintenance/replacement
and any consumed prior buffer are thus already debited once. Separately report
new rebuilt-contact load work; historical stored work cannot satisfy that test.

## Admission and stop decisions

Before executing any candidate dynamics, use the accessible potential and finite
load/storage capacities to bound surplus; preserve upper-bound failures. Exact
accounting feasibility requires positive whole AND post surplus, fresh rebuilt
contact output, all five arms funding their schedules, and a strictly greater
whole/post result than the independent envelope and the loop control. Report
all128 cases, failed controls, depletion, exergy and costs, not just certificates.

Independent interpreter and deterministic replay must match every rational
ledger and material/provenance endpoint. Regression fixtures must reject a
zero-work replacement claim and establish control endpoint accessibility.

Any positive certificate remains controlled-only under declared virtual prices.
Unknown natural transport, conversion, contact formation/release and constructor
establishment rates/costs are not zero. Natural-world admission remains CLOSED
until a separate, fully priced accessible primitive/formation law is registered
and audited; this protocol does not authorize a successful founder or world panel.
If no complete comparative certificate passes, stop BUFFER-01 under this law:
do not lower costs, alter cutoff/tapes/damage or extend its horizon.
