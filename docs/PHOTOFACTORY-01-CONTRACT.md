# PHOTOFACTORY-01: complete digital fabrication, conversion and renewal

Prospective October10,2026. A distinct isolated computational mechanism, not
biological work, a change to production Ora, or a retuning of a closed candidate.
The units and transitions below are explicitly installed digital physics, not
empirically calibrated material properties. This preflight asks whether a
completely specified paid fabricator can beat direct photofabrication. It does
not assume that programmable possibilities arise spontaneously.

## Constitutive rules versus apparatus

The environment has anonymous mutually contacting positions, finite neutral
raw particles and incident unit-exergy photons. It supplies no working devices,
constructor, controller, private charge or replenishment outside the finite
registered feeds. Position labels do not affect a transition; this deliberately
well-mixed digital law has no advantage from ordering contact positions.

Five device roles use two raw particles of their own declared type: C capture,
H contact housing, S storage, F fabricator, L load/work port. A device has one
unit of bound potential and a fresh identity. C,H,S,L are required for useful
output; F is the candidate's proposed construction apparatus. Raw particles
have no device function. Capture alone holds two carriers; paid working H and S
extend the local bank to six. H couples parts within one engine; independent
engines have separate banks and no transfer. All selected apparatus must be
maintained before use. Raw photofabrication is a constitutive reaction, not an
unpaid hidden machine: it is available to every arm at the stated price.

Every operation consumes uniquely identified energy tokens and creates equal
total unit energy in bound potential, carriers, heat or raised load work. New
tokens retain complete input ancestry. Every material identity remains either
raw or locked in exactly one working/broken device; release returns those same
particles. No raw material or energy is created by bookkeeping.

- Direct photofabrication: two raw particles plus three photons become a fresh
  device with one bound potential and two heat. All five roles can form this way.
- Capture: working maintained C,H consume two photons, yielding one local carrier
  and one heat, subject to capacity. There is no capture from raw particles.
- Fabrication: working maintained C,H,F spend two local carriers plus two raw
  particles to form ANY missing role, including F, with one potential and one
  heat. No repair target or successful policy is embedded in this reaction.
- Upkeep: one photon per device per epoch becomes heat; the device is maintained
  for that epoch. Formed but unpaid devices cannot perform their functions.
- Load: working maintained C,H,S,L spend four carriers to raise two separately
  identified unit-energy loads, plus two heat. Load work is an external conserved
  output stock, not a success score or policy reward. The finite load inventory
  is1200 initially unraised identities per arm; no formation device is omitted.
- Functional damage: one separately recorded tester work token per device becomes
  heat and disables that device. Its two particles and one potential remain
  locked. This paid external intervention is not ordinary natural damage.
- Release: two photons plus a broken device's one potential become three heat;
  its particles return to raw stock. Repair cannot use them before this payment.
- Expiry: all residual photons/carriers at epoch end become heat, retaining IDs
  and ancestry. Raised work remains. No pre-loss stored energy can count as fresh
  post-loss output.

The source provides576 photons in each of three epochs,1728 total. Initially
four raw particles of each of five types:20 conserved particles. Two full loss
events separate epochs; no role or functioning founder survives within an
engine. New epochs have fresh source IDs and exact exposure. Any reformed device
has a new identity and records its reused material and new work ancestry. Maximum
device damage tokens20 covers all arms; unused tester tokens remain separately
accounted, never accessible by the engine. Constitutive rules are not apparatus
that must reproduce; all actual apparatus roles above must be paid and renewed.

## Controlled feasibility and decisive comparisons

Before any stochastic world, execute explicitly authored feasible action
sequences. They are controlled reconstruction, NOT autonomous trajectories.
Their scheduling is external experimental assistance, not an installed actor.
No device-payback score prices that assistance at zero or admits autonomy.
Prospective autonomous dynamics, if ever justified, would use uniform blind
operation/engine/role choices among the declared local transitions, consuming
draws on invalid attempts too; exact draw layout, rates, fresh unscreened samples
and acceptance thresholds would require a separate frozen panel. No such actor
or successful policy is implemented here. The bound can reject the candidate
without supplying a successful founder or spending resources on those worlds.
Candidate: one engine; directly form and maintain C,H,F, then let F form and
maintain S,L. Capture/export maximally until no further complete load is funded.
After each full loss, release every broken part and repeat from raw particles.
Stationary simple control: one engine, direct C,H,S,L, no fabricator; otherwise
same capture/load/upkeep/release rules and source. Independent simple control:
two uncoupled such engines,288 photons each epoch each, disjoint particle IDs;
same total source, raw stock and output opportunities. No favorable seeds exist.

Full-shuffle control: all120 permutations of the candidate's five initial
anonymous contact labels. This changes the entire role-to-position ordering,
not functioning roles or prices. It is an initial-condition comparison, not a
free physical rearrangement after damage. All permutations are attainable from
raw stock and receive identical feed. Under this law they should be equivalent;
equal function falsifies an ordering advantage. A fabricator-disabled diagnostic
retains/maintains F but uses direct formation: no role cost is refunded. Verify
that a fabricated device actually depended on F in the candidate history.

Primary feasibility requires positive raised work minus all fabrication, upkeep
and release expenditure, both whole-life and in EACH fresh post-damage epoch.
Conversion/load heat is already charged by reducing raised output; report it
explicitly without double-debiting it as a second maintenance expenditure.
Tester work is separate assistance and must also be reported/subtracted in the
assistance-inclusive whole score. Positive payback is not a full energy surplus
over environmental source: conservation forbids energy generation. Same full
bills and payback definitions apply to every comparator, including useful work
and remaining stores. Require strict whole/post advantage over profitable simple
and full-shuffle controls before admission. One complete matched failure rejects
the candidate under this frozen law; do not alter prices/feeds or omit controls.

Independently interpret every event, energy balance, phase, device function,
material lock, ancestry and terminal payback without importing the producer.
Replay exactly; restored-source audit must match. Check all120 permutations,
both whole losses and every new role identity. Also prove the early stop: two
fabricator carriers require four source photons through capture, versus three
for direct formation. Maintenance/startup of F adds cost. This is an accessible
direct alternative, not a free constructor. No natural-world panel if dominated.
