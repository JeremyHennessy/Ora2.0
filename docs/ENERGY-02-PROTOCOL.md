template/body is retained. Recognize the supplied precursor at the next channel
position: pay1 from that precursor's activation in coupled modes, or actor
usable work in uncoupled mode. If bit mismatches the corresponding template
position, dissipate1 and keep material ready. Un templated mode still pays1 but
accepts any bit. A match becomes pending bound material; recognition while
already pending is unavailable and does not charge again.

Ligate pending material: the first atom joins the channel without a bond or
extra charge. Later joins require2 units from the incoming precursor (coupled)
or actor work (uncoupled); one becomes heat, the other becomes the new uniquely
source-linked structural bond. An unfunded pending atom remains bound. No
cross-precursor energy pool may pay recognition or ligation.

precursor activation can transfer2 endowment units (coupled), or actor work can
pay release1 plus endowment2 (uncoupled). Release spends1 heat; remaining
channel2 units transfer to a newly identified child. The channel's bond units
transfer to that child's bonds. Record template, producer, component roots,
all construction debits, birth bits and producer-funding classification.

material bits. No virtual child without executed release.
