# Ora strategy — evolve new learning rules from organism-native code

**Decision:** 2026-10-23  
**Scope:** Documentation-only strategic direction. This does not authorize a World launch, VM change, source/seed retune, local assistant mutation, observer deployment, new schedule, imported knowledge data or any scientific acceptance.

**Scientific partner:** [Independent evolvability research decision in AI-Research](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/AI-Research/blob/main/docs/artificial-life/ORA-ENDOGENOUS-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-DECISION-20261023.md).

## The decision

Make **evolution of useful information-handling behaviour** the primary science objective. Stop measuring progress by PR counts, simulator uptime, number of actor decisions, supplied learning modules or growth of the host assistant's memory database.

The running local Ollama assistant is a separate useful tool. It is not a naturally evolving digital organism. Don't merge its approved memory or model reasoning into scientific organism behaviour.

**Key gap:** Longitudinal01 already showed eight generations using a supplied ancestor, Learning81/Context90 proved narrow own-history use under supplied learning code, and Inheritance83 did not show beneficial inherited selection. Bigger models improved correct decisions without robust fully priced gains. PILOT74 full native original-owner qualification and persistent population launch remain separate unfinished engineering milestones.

**Selected hypothesis EKA-01:** Existing organism instruction primitives allow feedback, memory, self-write of its own code, paid COPY and division. Could *neutral, naturally arising code mutations* lead organisms to develop a more useful way of deciding what to remember and what to carry into their offspring? At this point the possible mechanism and its mutational attainability are only hypotheses.

## What to do first — EKA-0, read-only/no World

- Trace the actual Pressure71/Mobile54 VM instruction, COPY/mutation, SELFWRITE, feedback/memory, newborn reset, survival and material/energy laws. Preserve exact historical source identities.
- Determine whether there is **any realistically reachable path** from the unselected legal founder to an *actually expressed new information-handling phenotype* within ordinary alive/birth/exposure windows. Do not insert a hand-created successful genotype into a scientific population.
- Use independent source-only calculations or synthetic non-science fixtures to bound mutation supply, cell/lineage opportunities and the cost of sensing, reading, writing, copying and birth; strong compact reactive/fixed alternatives may be cheaper.
- Stop with NO-GO if expected phenotypes die before expression, have no informative exposure, or cannot cover their cost under the legal world. Do not solve the problem by adding free food, magic evolutionary targets, longer horizons, secretly tuned seeds or choosing a favored memory capacity.
- Review relevant AI-Research work and the original AgentTest failures as hypotheses/controls. The new result must not simply reproduce an existing result with a different name.
- Produce a short prospective EKA-1 experiment **proposal only**, including explicit causal counterfactuals, power/statistical feasibility, independent source/cost audits, unseen contexts, all-member denominators and correct scientific permission gates.

## If EKA-0 passes, request separate EKA-1 admission

A future finite experiment should compare natural-mutating complete populations to equally funded no-mutation, inherited-copy-disabled, effective SELFWRITE-disabled and experience-erasure controls, and disclose strong efficient fixed/reactive reference strategies. Follow naturally generated genomes through paid copy/birth and descendant action; test a previously absent functional routine across independently unseen environments and demonstrate attributable actual descendant advantage. Every failure, parent death, unborn candidate, physical resource and unexpressed mutation counts. Do not retrospectively modify closed worlds.

A **mutation**, **birth**, **personal learning**, **genomic self-write**, and **offspring advantage** are separate events. Never claim a novel evolved learning algorithm unless a new lineage mechanism is actually expressed, causally helpful, passed by paid inheritance, competitively advantageous and independently reproduced.
