## Decision changed by the research and calculation

The [author source on correlation-powered engines](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08506v2)
describes returning to a dynamical phase after input corruption. That is not a
witness for reconstructing a destroyed engine. The
[requisite-variety source](https://csc.ucdavis.edu/~cmg/compmech/pubs/tom.htm)
also warns that finite-state optimal engines do not simply attain the general
information bounds. The [correlation-aware thermodynamics source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01537v4)
supports reviewing joint/process information rather than single-symbol counts.
These are reviewed author abstracts, not a local replication of their complete
transition laws or a claim that the full papers establish no construction route.

**Admission remains denied, not a negative capability trial.** Formation,
memory/reset, transport, coupling, load conversion, upkeep, release and all-role
reconstruction costs remain UNKNOWN. No positive actual comparator endpoints or
raw-to-apparatus formation law are established. Do not set those costs to zero,
subtract two upper bounds to claim comparative advantage, or select only the24
positive opportunities for worlds. No controller or successful policy was added.

Next question is narrower: specify a complete paid memoryful converter and a
paid full-block stationary implementation on the identical finite source. Give
actual transition efficiencies and raw-to-functional renewal of every role,
then compute useful whole/post output. Without those witnesses, this remains
an unadmitted source direction. ADAPTATION's history-shuffle control must leave
