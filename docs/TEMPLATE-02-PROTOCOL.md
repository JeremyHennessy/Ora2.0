## Law and finite genesis

One well-mixed container, no spatial affinity, reward, chosen founder or supplied
polymer. Unique atoms have immutable bits and external genesis provenance.
Start with 32 single-monomer objects, 32 ready monomer atoms and 32 nutrients
(potential 8 each); all 96 bits are unscreened Python Random(seed) randrange(2).
Each object has 2 work in the funded regime, 0 in the paired starved regime.
Private accounts or a shared bank contain the same total work 64/0. Heat starts
at zero, bonds zero, waste empty. No food, material or work is replenished.

Generic ligation joins two distinct objects, total length <=4, consumes both and
charges 2 work. One new bond stores 1; 1 becomes heat. Private objects merge
their actual accounts, spending the first 2 units and transferring all remaining
units to the product. Shared-bank ligation charges its global bank, products
have no private work. Retain both consumed parents and their immutable birth
records; all material identities remain unique. This is installed assembly,
not an evolved constructor, membrane or energy-allocation policy.

Keep TEMPLATE-01 production/contact/decay/regeneration prices unchanged. Copy
requires length >=2, n ready atoms and 3n+1 work: n recognition+2(n-1) ligation+
1 release+2 child endowment. Store n-1 bonds, release 2n heat, transfer the last
2 debited work units to the child; retain the parent. Contact costs up to 2
(read then process). Terminal-bit complement and length >=2 convert a nutrient
to waste, return 3 work and 5 additional heat; W1 cannot convert. Missing or
nonbinding contacts still spend available work. Neutral decay releases all
bonds and private work to heat, atoms to waste. Reclaim one waste atom for
1 work/1 heat into the ready reserve. No free recycling or atom duplication.

Track individual work units: immutable genesis-unit IDs and nutrient-derived
unit IDs naming the contacting actor, nutrient and tick. Spend FIFO, merge in
selected left/right order, append returns. Shared bank retains its own FIFO.
Every current work unit has one account; history is informational, not another
owner. Record debited units and endowed/returned units in every event. Heat
cannot fund an action. At every step conserve per-bit material and total
work+heat+bonds+8*remaining nutrients, plus exact material/work provenance.

## Frozen neutral generator and five arms

Fresh unscreened seeds 128..143: 16 independent initializations. After 96 bit
draws freeze 128 quintuples: action randrange(8), four selectors randrange(65536)
for owner, partner, ready/waste and nutrient. Sorted string-ID candidate pools;
modulo selects owner then a distinct partner. Ready order is sorted and rotated
by its selector; templating greedily takes the first remaining matching atom for
each bit, untemplated production takes first n without bit recognition. No
post-outcome selection. Nutrient/waste uses its corresponding selector.

Actions 0/1 ligate, 2 copy, 3 contact, 4 decay, 5 reclaim, 6/7 idle. One proposal
per tick; retain all 128 events even after starvation. IDs g0..g31 at genesis,
o{tick} for every successful ligation/copy, never reused. No deferred retry or
extra action. Impossible/unfunded ligation/copy/reclaim costs zero: a favorable
modeling assumption explicitly distinct from funded contact/paid ghost costs.

Arms share material, total initial work and proposals:

- Active: template matching, private work accounts.
- Fixed: same physical rule via independent bit comparison; exact active match.
- Paid ghost: eligible funded template copy pays 3n+1 entirely to heat, no child.
- Un templated: same production price/endowment, no sequence recognition; retain
  producer provenance but no template-parent claim. Chance matches count.
- Shared bank: matching template rule, global financing; child endowment routes
  back to the global bank. Work escapes object decay, an explicit subsidy null.

Per-eligible copy prices match, not later trajectories or total spending. No
cross-model rate pooling with prior studies or disguised free access.

## Endpoints and decision

sequence-matching its producer, whose entire 3n+1 debit was paid with work units
captured by that producer itself, subsequently converts a nutrient. Producer
must be an internally born polymer; no initial polymers exist. Count distinct
functional children and conversion events separately. A matching untemplated
child qualifies; direct template-parent labels do not decide the endpoint.

fully own-resource-funded child that subsequently functions. Distinguish first
