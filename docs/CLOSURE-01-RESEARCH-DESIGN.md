# CLOSURE-01 — Toward endogenous organization, not long-lived sprites

**Status:** unimplemented original research proposal, 2026-10-08. This is not a proven AI architecture, a living organism, or a report of a world-first mechanism. Do not merge existing Ora2.0 substrates merely to fill a checklist. No prior Ora/AgentTest method is a design input.

## Why this is the next independent scientific question

Four distinct existing observations remain confined to separate models:
- AL01 random catalytic graphs demonstrate some constituent-recovery feedback after a targeted reaction disruption; no boundaries or actual heritable organisms.
- AL02 bytecode copiers demonstrate executed causal inheritance of existing instructions; bodies and reproductive semantics are host-defined.
- AL07 confirms 1,621 byte-copy lineages from the same AL02 event stream; not causal individuality across spatially distributed structures.
- AL03-NICHE and its AL03-MAINT follow-up demonstrate a preprogrammed resource-economy and that scheduled external supply plus an occupancy cap were confounding perceived survival. 48/48 fed worlds persisted under even the heaviest tested idle upkeep, with mean population down to 5.8; **this is not endogenous metabolism**.

The question is whether we can detect a **localized system that produces and actively restores some of the processes/boundaries required for its own ongoing existence**, with no engine-side `repair(entity)` function.

## Scientific assumptions and prior evidence

Most useful source comparisons from verified [AI-Research Pass 6](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/AI-Research/blob/909d90a438273636d3ce3dee8a5880126a7bfb92/docs/artificial-life/34-pass6-research-handoff.md):
- [Stepney 2025 E2](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0298): autonomy/agency/open-ended adaptation are proposed **requirements**, not satisfied by any simple toy world.
- [Liu & Sumpter 2018](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA118.003795): reaction-network amplification under a constructed chemical universe is not a self-produced compartment.
- [Stringmol 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_a_00180): decay and allocation rules can accidentally select unproductive immortality and giant inert programs.
- [Stringmol semantic closure 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.1033): some self-encoding copier/expressor organizations remain viable under mutations, but self-maintaining interpretation can also collapse.
- [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2): individual causal organization may span spatially disconnected patterns; simple region overlap can misidentify individuality.

These works do **not** provide a proven recipe or independent reproduction by Ora2.0. Scientific organization/biochemical substrate definitions remain contested.

## Original project hypothesis CLOSURE-H01 (untested)

Given local reaction rules with conserved substrate and *internally consumable repair pathways*, some unscreened initial conditions may form and sustain a localized **functional boundary** whose turnover and damage recovery depend causally on an internally maintained network of reactions; identical total material, but passively stable and externally propped-up structures, should fail this full battery.

Falsifier: stabilization/recovery still occurs when the nominated internal restorative cycle is disabled, or results are matched by a passive attractor/external timed rebuild, or no unseeded organization satisfies the preregistered operational criteria.

### Rival hypothesis CLOSURE-H02 (stronger negative)

Apparent long-run spatial organization in the candidate physics is explainable entirely by:
1. diffusion/conservation mathematical attractors;
2. externally prescribed boundary impermeability;
3. unlimited resource injection or engine immorality;
4. the observer's post-hoc definition of what counts as an individual.

A result consistent with H02 is valuable. We should not decorate it with biological vocabulary.

## Candidate substrate A — non-neural reaction/diffusion chemistry

**Uncommitted concept, not parameter freeze.** Start from a small, deterministic and/or stochastic **2D spatial lattice** with globally conserved or explicitly tracked **S** substrate, catalyst candidates **X/Y/R**, replaceable boundary constituents **M**, waste **W**, and externally bounded inflow/heat. The programmer specifies elementary reactions, transport/diffusion and conservation laws. **No object list of living cells, protected hit points, task reward, genotype, or automatic reconstitution.**

Key design tension: if an M barrier and R repair cycle are hand-authored then we have designed an apparent organism. For a true *origin-of-organization* test, select reaction graphs/initial states from a preregistered distribution **without screening for viable patterns**. A separate authored-catalyst calibration may validate the assay but cannot be counted as emergence.

Possible mechanism: catalyst networks produce M from S; local M changes transport probabilities and reduces loss of reactive members; X/Y/R participate in replacing vulnerable catalyst species while paying material costs. M degrades, so existing M cannot persist without active replacement. **The effect of M on transport is itself authored physics** and must be openly compared to a matched passive barrier or reduced-diffusion null.

Do not assign rigid compartments at startup. A candidate region/individual must be detected independently from local molecule flux and tested for stability when members are removed or boundaries change.

## Candidate substrate B — process organization without an explicit chemical analog

Independent comparator: a finite graph of local processes consumes limited externally provided event budgets to create/maintain its own *runtime-valid* dependencies under decay. No Python interpreter/source modification; process graph edges are inert sandbox data. Compare active repair of repair nodes against protected and externally rebooted process graphs. Do not call generic fault-tolerant software living simply because it recovers.

**Do not integrate A+B unless evidence shows an identifiable scientific advantage.** The second substrate exists to challenge an overfit chemical metaphor, not to add complexity for its own sake.

## Minimum preregistered counterfactual panel

1. **Unseeded versus seeded positive control:** random spatial sources/molecules versus a deliberately assembled known productive organization. Keep these denominators separate.
2. **Internal-repair knockout:** suppress a required internal reaction/repair path **without creating or destroying free resources**. Remove equal-cost nonessential pathway in a blind sham.
3. **Repair-of-repair:** damage both boundary material M and internal regeneration components R; determine whether active internal reactions reconstitute *R itself*, not only a wall or an arbitrary image.
4. **Passive-attractor null:** same local diffusion/material conservation but no internal productive coupling.
5. **Externally stabilized null:** matched artificial barrier/enforced protection of equal cost. Do not mistake externally pinned lifetime for autopoiesis.
6. **Resource denial and matched inflow:** restore, reduce or remove external substrate flux, account for supply and work. Total mass/energy conservation must hold within chosen abstract physics.
7. **Held-out perturbations:** varying locations, intensities, topological interruptions and time-varying supply; the viability criterion and segmentation thresholds are frozen separately from holdout.
8. **Observer-relabeling audit:** multiple independent region/causal-flow detectors must not call an inert oscillator or mere diffusion pocket an individual.

## What a future valid outcome would look like

Report *independently*:
- survival/recovery after intervention (not just peak population or area);
- **constitutive turnover**: boundary and internal machinery replaced, with replacement processes documented;
- **mechanistic dependency**: restore fails under internal mechanism knockout but not under same-cost sham;
- net material and abstract energy accounting under bounded inflow, no secret replenishment;
- individuality ambiguity at fission/merger and detector false-positive rates;
- repeatability on multiple unselected world seeds and source/physics changes.

A positive localized repair result alone is **not** proof of biological life, spontaneous heredity, agency, consciousness, higher intelligence or open-ended evolution. A finite-memory world cannot empirically prove infinite novelty.

## Gate before writing any simulated organization

This document intentionally does **not** fix reaction rates, grid size, number of entities, seeds or a desired organism shape. Before any implementation:
1. Independently review primary papers/methods and reject attractive but physically invalid assumptions.
2. Precommit a *specific* substrate and exact transition semantics, conservation checks and computational budget in a **new protocol commit before held-out evaluation**.
3. Define pre-registered segmentation and causal falsifiers that an inert attractor should fail.
4. Build a tiny deterministic conservation/damage calibration and prove the independent evaluator on adversarial counterexamples.
5. Run all randomly sampled initial conditions including extinction, no-pattern and incoherent states; do not select a visually appealing survivor to start an "organism" history.
6. Only if meaningful, compare a rival substrate or consider narrow combination with verified executed heredity in another **separately authorized, reversible experiment**.
