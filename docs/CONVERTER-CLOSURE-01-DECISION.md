**October 11, 2026. Targeted source/admission decision, not a simulation result.**

Question: does the reviewed correlation-powered ratchet supply the complete
paid converter needed after CORRELATION-CAPACITY-01? Decision: **no**. Stop this
specific driven architecture as the primary whole-system candidate. This does
not falsify correlated-input conversion or the paper's narrower findings.

The exact reviewed source is Boyd, Mandal and Crutchfield,
[arXiv:1606.08506v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.08506), especially III.2 and
Appendix A.1. The full HTML explains that tape advance is driven; interaction

The three-state transition matrices in Appendix B are therefore insufficient
for the autonomous whole-system experiment. Synchronization of internal state
after input corruption does not replace damaged apparatus.

Only these specific sections and the displayed Appendix B matrices were
reviewed. This is not independent reproduction of the source model, verification
of its complete physical implementation, or a review of every cited paper.
No gross work, whole-life surplus, actual comparator profit or post-loss useful
output has been measured here. Those values remain UNKNOWN, not zero.

## Next candidate decision card: AUTONOMOUS-CONTACT-01

This is a **distinct architecture proposal**, not an admitted complete mechanism
or a renamed implementation of the driven ratchet. There is no controller
activation, learning policy, LLM or new experimental law in this change.

- Question: can local, reversible contact exchange itself pay for movement and
  switching while a raw-fed system renews every required role and retains more
  useful work than an accessible stationary block converter?
- Distinctive causal capability: contact progression is a resource-coupled state
  transition within the apparatus, rather than an imposed one-way clock. Its
  possible advantage is avoiding external advance support; this is unmeasured.
- Smallest decisive prototype: one finite contact interval plus a paid return
  cycle, candidate and stationary block alternative on the exact same feed.
  Record direction, contact identity, energy before/after, material state,
  work/heat, and transition ancestry. No long sequence or large panel first.
- Required apparatus inventory: capture/coupling element, memory if used,
  transport/switching element, load port, and every construction/release
  capability. Constitutive transitions must be distinguished from apparatus.
  A role cannot disappear from the bill by being called part of the substrate.
- Complete bill: raw/source preparation, formation, initialization, coupling,
  transport, sensing/state where used, reset/return, release, upkeep, load,
  waste/heat and post-loss renewal. All presently unresolved costs are UNKNOWN.
- Controls: actual stationary block converter, viable independent conversion,
  paid history shuffle with incoming resource fixed, and function-disabled
  diagnostics. A memoryless-only null or altered-input shuffle is insufficient.
- Accept only positive complete whole-life and fresh post-loss useful surplus,
  all-role causal renewal, a profitable strongest simple control and an audited
  advantage. Independently reproduce before any self-maintenance acceptance.
- **Current decision: named missing parameter.** The indispensable missing item
  is a thermodynamically consistent contact/return transition law linked to a
  raw-accessible formation route. Resolve it by constructing an explicit
  reversible transition table with potentials and source debits, then a complete
  raw-to-role renewal ledger. A transition probability alone is not that law.
  If neither can be specified from constitutive physics without installing
  machinery or favorable arbitrary prices, reject this proposal too and choose
  another mechanism. Do not generate another capacity census in its place.
