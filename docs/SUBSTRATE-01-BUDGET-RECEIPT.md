These differing native scripts and installed access rules prohibit a
head-to-head fitness ranking. The interface effect is programmed nutrient
conversion through a carrier. It is fully reproduced by a fixed table and
externally supplied conversion; the ghost lacks the only installed access
function, so zero capture is expected. No constructive superiority, closed
production network or independent self-maintenance was established.

The marker is an especially crude trace: it is written after the first
consumption, even if more nutrient remains. It therefore suppresses available
resource contacts in the productive case as well as after renewal. A foreign
marker produces the same suppression. These deliberately exposed weaknesses
are retained, not repaired by changing marker semantics after viewing outputs.
The fixed reflex explains every trace-active history. This does not refute all
environmental traces, and does not demonstrate learned reliability/forgetting.

**Decision (predeclared conditional on valid ledgers):** carry forward the
interface-first design because a local paid conversion is a simpler causal
measurement. Keep trace and fixed counterparts as separately specified
competitors. Neither kernel is admitted to repair, memory utility, heredity
or life claims by this calibration. No new stochastic pilot was run.

## Important cost and provenance limits

The common ledger charges construction, local reading/processing and erasure.
Carrier retention is passive with zero recurrent work charge and four bound
material; no retention-time adaptation, maintenance economy or true
thermodynamics is modeled. Nutrient renewal transfers existing escrow stock,
not an undeclared injection. No-field avoids construction costs and is an
explicitly unmatched baseline. Ghost and active can diverge in subsequent
conversion/processing totals; only recorded per-event and named fixture
equalities are established. All native case scripts were authored in advance.

Labels internal/external identify paid fixture operations and foreign
interventions. Internal in this kernel does not mean autonomous endogenous
origin: a researcher invokes the local proposals. No natural exposure or
random seed was inferred from these events.
