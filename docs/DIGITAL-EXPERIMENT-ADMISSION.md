## Immediate priority: establish full-cost ordinary viability

Before a new reproduction or mutation population comparison, derive and record a
complete lifecycle budget, then validate the disclosed ordinary route with the
same generic endpoint evaluator used for the prospective experiment.

The budget must include:

1. Paid founder assembly and every code/state role; a longer supplied program
   increases construction and copying costs.
2. Actual successful and unsuccessful acquisition instructions, control flow,
   first-copy selector storage, every accepted/rejected sampling draw, and all
   words required to complete a nursery.
3. Division costs, offspring startup transfer, parent retention, refusal paths,
   energy-cap overflow and timing-dependent resource accessibility.
4. The descendant's ability to acquire resources and reproduce after birth,
   including actual exposure to released or changed resources.

A positive average cycle balance is necessary in a repeated-cycle argument,
but is not sufficient to establish birth or descendant viability. Show the
actual complete paid path, including transient shortages and scheduler effects.
Use analytical bounds and small disclosed software fixtures before a primary
population panel. Freeze scientific seeds, controls, gates and falsifiers before
primary execution. Stop admission on failure; retain the failure and unused
reserves. Never install a prepared successful learner as an emerged organism.

The SINGLECOPY09 diagnostic spends11 energy per successful acquisition/copy
cycle against average10 intake, with additional first-copy overhead. Its zero
birth result rejects that particular ordinary configuration. It does not
invalidate earlier digital reproduction. A different acquisition-before-copy
program is a prospective supplied configuration with its own full budget;
neither its viability nor its discovery is assumed.

## Separate scientific questions

| Question | Required admission and evidence |
|---|---|
| Useful reproductive inheritance | Paid births and descendant viability; all naturally generated lineages and deaths; useful function transmitted without installing a selected winner |
| Mutation-operator comparison | Viable ordinary reference; disclosed neutral variation and matched comparison scope; complete error/copy ancestry and unconditional outcomes |
| Within-life learning | Personally informative paid experience and repeated useful action opportunities; fixed-response, memory-disabled and own-history-disrupted controls; genuinely unseen conditions and recorded durable experience channels |
| Sustained evolution | Functional inheritance, heritable variation and measured differential reproductive success across generations; neither births nor genome diversity alone |

Reproduction is not a universal prerequisite for a bounded within-life learning
experiment. Learning and reproductive viability may advance in parallel under
their own prospective contracts. An authored feedback-responsive witness proves
measurement or instruction reachability only. Feedback sensitivity, memory
instructions, counterfactual probes and installed mutation mechanisms do not
prove learned benefit, independent discovery or evolution.

## Interpret copying overhead precisely

Distinguish necessary work under the registered substrate from extra machinery
used to match a particular causal comparison. For example, charging faithful
copying for unused random draws can isolate an operator comparison, but that
matched bill is not the general minimum cost of digital reproduction. Preserve
the current registered bill. Any differently priced or organized mechanism
requires a justified, newly prospective model, explicit controls and fresh
provenance; never lower historical prices or silently rescore old results.

## Development discipline and reporting

Prefer resolving a demonstrated dependency within a suitable substrate before
introducing another world version. A new constitutive law is justified only by
an explicit missing affordance or measurement need, separately reviewed and
registered. Do not impose a calendar breakthrough or use PR/test counts as
scientific progress.

After each block report the supplied substrate, organism-generated behavior,
complete resource/ancestry record, all failures and unconditional denominators,
causal comparison, claim-specific decision and single next dependency. Keep
specification conformance, accounting and scientific acceptance separate.
