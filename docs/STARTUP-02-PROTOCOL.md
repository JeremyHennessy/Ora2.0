## Installed law, generic organization and complete costs

Actions are activate, recharge, start, extend, release, capture. Indices select
monomer modulo24, food modulo64, live chain modulo its current sorted-ID count
and live product modulo its sorted-ID count. The fifth index is recorded but
unused. Fixed raw atoms/foods are selected even when consumed/unavailable;
live-chain/product selection does not favor any bit, length, function or outcome.
All attempts and unavailable cases remain recorded. No predetermined target length
or assembly container exists. Chain objects are created only by paid start/extend.

Activation/recharge can address a free or live-chain monomer; released-product
atoms are inaccessible. Empty activation requires local0; partial recharge
requires0<local<3 and is disabled in topup_off. Pay2 work plus one unused food8,
store3-local new potential units, dissipate10 minus stored units as heat. Before
tick128 the payer is the finite external bank. At/after128, only external mode
retains that bank; other modes must use an actual product's private work2 and
complementary food. Product length>=2 is required. Food-withdrawn seals all unused
food at128, without removing it from the ledger. No bank is replenished.

Start on a free charged monomer consumes1 potential as heat. Except association_off,
it creates a new singleton chain, transferring that atom from free material.
Association_off pays recognition but forms nothing. Extend chooses an existing
chain and a free monomer with local3: recognition1 to heat, ligation2 with one
unit retained as a bond and one as heat. Consume the old chain into a newly
identified longer chain, preserving ordered components and its entire paid ancestry.
Insufficient charge or absent participant consumes nothing. No bit selectivity.

Release chooses any chain length>=2 with at least3 pooled residual potential:
1 unit to heat,2 to a new product's private account; no target length condition.
Ghost pays the identical formation/release/endowment debit but heats those2 units.
Remaining local potential stays sequestered on the product atoms and is counted.
The full construction debit is3n+1: n recognition,2(n-1) ligation,1 release,2 endowment.
All extra activation/recharge costs, food/waste, unused charge and heat are also
counted; surplus is never refunded or relabeled as productive work.

Capture requires length>=2, terminal bit complementary to the selected unused
food and actual private work2. Consume work2+food8, create3 work units, heat7;
capacity equals current product length and overflow becomes heat. This is an
installed function, not discovered intelligence. Full empty reactivation of a
bound monomer is allowed, as in STARTUP-01's law; therefore topup_off need not
be a zero-release control in this different encounter panel. Test its outcomes
rather than asserting partial top-up is universally necessary.

Each atom/unit/object ID is unique. Histories are immutable structural births;
an extension/release has the consumed chain as parent, not a reproductive parent.
Mutable live work/potential accounts never rewrite structural histories. Track
every created unit's payer, donor and the two actual input-work parents. A release
is fully captured-resource-funded only if every construction debit/endowment
unit arose from activation/recharge with both input work units directly created
by captures. Report its actual funders and post-withdrawal paid capture separately.
Do not call that resource ancestry copying, descendants reconstructing a function,
transmitted information or reproduction. Raw bit order is generated by encounters.
