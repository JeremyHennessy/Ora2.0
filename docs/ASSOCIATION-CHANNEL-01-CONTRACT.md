# ASSOCIATION-CHANNEL-01: finite reversible recipient prerequisite

Prospective implementation contract, October 11, 2026. This is one isolated

## Exact question and boundary

Can an explicit finite third constituent receive a pair's binding-energy drop
and return that energy on the reverse transition, without an invisible sink,
overfilling its state space or using a direction-selecting constructor?

The restricted fixture has three distinct material identities: two pair members
and one recipient. Pair members have zero incoming relative kinetic energy.
Their potential is zero when free and `-B` when bound, where caller-specified
`B` is an exact positive rational energy scale. The recipient has finite integer
excitation `n` in `0..C`, energy `n*B`, and finite positive capacity `C`.
Total represented energy is `(n - bound)*B`. A contact coordinate is explicit.
No force, continuous position, momentum, collision probability or rate is
inferred from this discrete state model.

At contact the same reversible rule handles both directions:

- Free pair, `n<C`: bind the pair and increment recipient excitation.
- Bound pair, `n>0`: release the pair and decrement recipient excitation.
- Otherwise: retain the entire state and record the reason for no transition.

Away from contact the rule is the identity. Applying the rule twice must return
the exact original material/state record. There is no automatic recipient removal,
cooling, reset or functional-component construction. An externally prepared
contact is fixture assistance; its accessibility/cost is unresolved. Numerical
test energy scales are arithmetic fixtures, not admitted fabrication prices.
Capacity is a represented limit, not evidence of a freely fabricated recipient.

relative kinetic energy and prepared contact. Preserve the intermediate bound
state and final state. Also retain one full-recipient forward refusal, one
empty-recipient reverse refusal and one no-contact refusal. No seeds, parameter
sweep, reservoir reset, incoming source replacement or natural worlds.

Acceptance of this **kernel only** requires exact energy and identity conservation,
finite capacity, byte-equivalent state after reversal, correct refusals, and an
independent interpreter that imports no worker. That interpreter must reject

claimed useful work and event direction. Replay from an archived source copy

Fail and preserve evidence on any violation. Correcting a software defect requires
a new exact source freeze and fresh evidence directory. Do not overwrite a run.
