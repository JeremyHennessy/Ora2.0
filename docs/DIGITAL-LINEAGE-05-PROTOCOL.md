# DIGITAL-LINEAGE-05 prospective endogenous lineage comparison

Frozen October11,2026 before primary execution. This is a new comparison under
unchanged DIGITAL-DESCENT-04-v1 / Context03 laws, not an extension or rescoring of
any closed experiment. No genome selection, founder reinstallation or successful
policy insertion. Historical exploratory Descent04 lineages motivate the question
but supply no primary genome, seed or accepted outcome.

Question: can variation in naturally paid births transmit an acquisition behavior
that neither supplied ordinary route implements, while retaining reproduction and
improving population outcomes under the same finite opportunities?

Distinctive necessary capability: a naturally born non-ancestor must successfully
use both intake ports at distinct remote sites, including a port inconsistent with
the supplied position-parity route, and transmit that realized capability through
an exact paid copy. Constant port0 cannot implement both ports; parity cannot
implement the inconsistent choice. Genotype difference or repeated ordinary
acquisition alone does not meet this endpoint. This measures realized inherited
competence, not within-life learning or sustained evolution.

Candidate: the entire unscreened mutation-enabled population descended from the
supplied seven-triple ancestor. Comparators: faithful copies of that same ancestor,
faithful fixed-port0 mobile ancestor, and copying-disabled original ancestor.
Ancestor triples are exactly [[0,0,0],[6,0,0],[10,0,0],[12,0,0],[4,1,1],
[13,1,0],[7,0,0]]. Fixed replaces only the first triple with [4,0,0]. All programs,
mutation/copy primitives, scheduler, sensors and environmental ports are supplied.
All subsequent genomes remain sandboxed interpreted integer data. No selection,
repair, feedback policy, novelty reward, teacher, external memory or host access.

Smallest decisive panel: seeds25400..25407, four arms per seed, horizon1536
scheduler transitions or extinction, checkpoint at min(64,extinction). Common
exogenous stock/site keys per seed; outcomes are never copied between arms.
Seeds25408..25415 reserved and unexecuted. No winner substitution or tuning.

Complete bill: each founder21 code +16 local state material units and37 formation

Finite512 material,64 food packets each20 energy, total1420 including constructor.
Every instruction costs1; COPY costs an additional3 for three individually traced

sensor or message operation pays its instruction. Children start zero experience
at parent position. Resource overflow becomes heat; death releases owned material.
No replenishment or new energy. Same fair round-robin scheduler/population32 cap,
typed checkpoint reconstruction and full interpreter as the reviewed source.

Endpoint evaluates every actual birth and every action, retaining deaths and
unqualified lineages. For a born actor, collect successful remote ACT(position,
port) counts. A qualifying parent has a literal birth genome different from the
supplied ancestor, at least4 remote successes, at least two distinct remote sites
with both ports represented, and at least one successful non-parity choice.
A qualifying direct child has EXACTLY that parent's birth genome (all21 words),
at least4 remote successes, successes at those same two sites with the same ports,
and itself causes at least one paid birth. The parent-to-child birth certificate
must pass the independent interpreter. Any two-site witness meeting these rules
counts; record all witnesses, not a best lineage. Self-writing cannot silently
qualify as inherited copying: compare actual birth genomes and log executed writes.

Prepared positive and impossible witness inventories use this SAME endpoint.
They verify software measurement reachability only; they are not world histories,
organism achievements, primary ancestors or target genomes. Full event integrity
must first pass the separately implemented Descent04 interpreter; the endpoint
does not replace it.

Prospective gates: (1) ordinary readiness: every seed has at least one faithful
ordinary arm with remote successes>=4 and descendant-caused births>=1;
(2) endogenous transmission: at least two independent mutation seeds contain
a qualifying pair, faithful ordinary and copying-disabled arms contain none,
and all copying-disabled populations have zero births;
(3) paired benefit: on EVERY seed mutation remote successes and births are each
>= the better ordinary value for that metric, with at least one strict improvement.
All three are required for this comparison's scoped positive result. Gate failure
is preserved; readiness failure classifies an underexposed comparison, not failed
self-maintenance. Even success does not prove learning, sustained selection,
open-endedness, repair, intelligence or consciousness. No stage advance from
infrastructure alone. Mechanical whole/fresh work and damage remain null.
