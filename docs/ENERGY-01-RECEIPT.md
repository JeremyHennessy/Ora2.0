## Decision and next priority

**Paid capture feasibility passes; reproduction sufficiency does not.** Under
the explicit one-unit-per-body-atom storage hypothesis, lengths 2/3/4 hold at
most 2/3/4 usable units, below unchanged copy debits 7/10/13. This law has no copying
or reconstruction action; their absence is by construction and is not an
experimental test of reproduction. The capacity bound and nonpositive balanced
drift reject advancing this mechanism alone as a sufficient reproduction
candidate. Do not tune storage, prices, food or horizon to defeat that result.

Next highest-value science question: separately preregister feasibility of
**paid activated precursors and incremental reaction-energy coupling**, instead
of requiring an entire production debit to accumulate in a private body account.
Track who paid to activate each precursor, all stored potential/heat/waste,
recognition/ligation/release/endowment costs and atom ancestry. Compare inert
precursors, cost-matched ghost production, producer-derived versus externally
supplied activation and subsidy withdrawal. This is an independent prospective
hypothesis, not a selected successful organism or a final architecture. Check
accounting/possibility before any fresh neutral opportunity panel.
