## Why build this?

Ora's long-term ambition is not to make organisms rediscover everything independently. Human knowledge, existing algorithms, useful published research and eventually another organism's observations could become **information sources**. But using a lookup or a local LLM is not itself proof that the organism learned, evolved or invented the referenced algorithm.

At the October 23 checkpoint:
- Separate local assistant: working signed/approved memory and native retrieval; retention for new notes and the sealed 36-call project-work pilot still pending. This is a different runtime, not the scientific organism universe.
- Scientific population: bounded reproduction and finite ecology exist; no continuous scientific World or beneficial evolved learning capacity proven.
- Learning81: a scoped own-experience decision benefit under supplied architecture, but retained-resource advantage not met.
- Context90: scoped previously learned information retrieval at capacity q8, with no full paid N/O/E advantage.
- PILOT74: additional preparation for finite same-population continuation and native custody is under development. The complete production qualification and supervised persistent activation are still unproven.

## Proposed architectural separation

**A. Curated source catalog (host-side, offline and read-only).**
Use reviewed AI-Research publications/metadata and approved documents first. Each compact capsule must retain item ID, claim/type, source/version/digest, review/rights status, contradictory evidence, permitted scope and external translation provenance. A local Ollama model can suggest a summary but cannot self-approve it.

**B. Restricted research-to-organism interface (future design).**
Do not feed free-form papers to the current bytecode organism and call that comprehension. Design one narrow, encoded, source-neutral lookup/observation channel it can actually parse, with explicit query/CPU/storage/energy costs and refusal/null records. No shell, arbitrary network, repository access, host execution, organism control or privileged hidden targets. Query responses are data, never instructions.

**C. Native organism experience (already a separate experimental question).**
Its own sensing/action/feedback history belongs to the actor and pays the ordinary costs. Never silently merge this with the host assistant's approved memory or AI-Research curation.

**D. Cultural transmission (later).**
Only after independent K2/K3 knowledge-use results, consider bounded in-world messages: a teacher sends a claimed observation; a recipient may accept, test or ignore it. Trace who first acquired it, who transmitted it, what the receiver did, and all charges. Keep transmitted knowledge separate from inherited source code.

## Proposed milestones and stop gates

| Milestone | Smallest deliverable | Evidence needed | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K0 — Traceable library schema | Reviewed schema plus one safely sourced, versioned example and cost/permission audit | Provenance, rights, scope and clear distinction between authored versus acquired | **Proposed; may draft now** |
| K1 — Offline deterministic librarian | Restricted, exact-hash read-only snapshot with reject paths | Retrieval and corruption tests without VM or service activation | **Future optional engineering** |
| K2 — First paid organism read | One prospectively registered encoded information opportunity | Real same-organism query, access, action, budget, null and fixed/reactive controls | **Not admitted** |
| K3 — Learn to seek knowledge | Resource-aware self-chosen queries on unseen tasks | Improved actual choice/return versus equal-access, equally funded references | **Not demonstrated** |
| K4 — Cultural learning | Source-attributed messages between independently alive organisms | Receiver benefit with no-teaching/shuffled/self-experience controls and full communication costs | **Speculative** |
| K5 — Accumulating digital culture | Long-term verified accumulation, correction and transfer | Durable real improvement independent of model-host help and scientist-selected winners | **Speculative** |

Acceptance is **not** a feature flag. At each stage use prospective task/source/cohort/controls, full actual costs, death/failure/NULL denominators, independent literal/source review and unchanged reserved banks. If relevant source information is already readable through ordinary current cues or costs too much to use, reject that experiment before new Worlds.

## Non-negotiable integration boundaries

1. Do not block, retune or replace the existing PILOT74 native/world-continuation work or the approved local assistant runtime, its store, exclusive writer and memory-transfer checks.
2. Do not merge approved human documents, local assistant memory, hereditary code and organism personal/cultural experience into one unlabelled memory database.
3. Do not modify historical experiments, laws, costs, seeds, protected reserves, scientific outcomes, stage credit or the read-only Observer to make a knowledge test pass.
4. Do not enable paid APIs, external ingestion, unattended scrapers, extra schedules, self-hosted GitHub runners, credentials, networked organism tools or any new persistent service without separate explicit authorization.
5. Do not leak private notes/keys, data archives or machine identifiers into GitHub. Retrieved text is always untrusted data; no embedded instruction can grant authority.
6. No guarantee of consciousness, open-ended evolution or improved fitness follows from availability of research.
