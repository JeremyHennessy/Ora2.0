unchanged by activation/refund, waste/regeneration or binding/dissociation.

regeneration the whole-life balance cannot exceed-3. Any positive whole store
therefore requires at least six net captures, including regenerated-fuel use.
Binding5/6 additionally debits supplied structural capital drawdown1/2; final
W must exceed that debit. Binding releases heat, not free usable work.
Before damage, a bound pair with W>=2 needs at least four net captures;
retaining one of four fuel objects at that point requires regeneration. This
algebra is a consequence of the law, not a new frozen score or a claim that
regeneration is impossible. Reverse waste and thermal fluctuations are included

Thus stability and source accessibility must be investigated jointly; assembly
counts or refunded construction alone cannot establish profitable maintenance.
