## Question and deliberately installed physics

Can a finite candidate require a physically present, paid, system-produced
constructor C for interface I assembly, while independently logging what
produces C? This addresses INTERFACE-01's privileged direct-bank builder.

At a stationary site, four single-occupancy slots hold A (C's producer), C
(constructor), I (nutrient access), and D (inert matched material). Each object
has mass4, zero stored usable energy, a never-reused ID, birth event and live
same-site catalyst ID. The fixed law installs A->C, C->A, C->I and C->D.
It is a researcher-designed catalytic cycle, not discovered reaction physics,
a genome, an autonomous controller or evidence of organizational emergence.

All construction needs a live catalyst, an empty product slot,4 precursor P
and2 usable work W;4P becomes the object,2W becomes heat. D is inert but uses
the same C gate and construction price as A/I. There is no active-law direct
P->C or P->I construction. Catalysts are not consumed or cloned into the
product. A catalyst surviving an event is not a newly produced component.

Contact pays1W for readout, then1W for processing, even with an empty nutrient
slot or absent/nonfunctional I. If only1W remains, readout exhausts it and
processing fails. With functional I and nutrient S, one S (mass1, potential8)
becomes waste1, work3 and heat5. Successful complete contact therefore has
net +1W and +7heat. At W<2 conversion cannot start: an energetic resource is
not already usable work. Passive retention costs0W, explicitly a favorable
physical assumption; this is not a fully paid maintenance model.

Decay retires one object ID into4 waste, with no P return or W debit. A paid
impairment instead requires1W, retires the selected object into4 waste, and
adds1heat. Missing-target/unaffordable attempts must be logged, not replaced
with a later favorable target. Free waste-to-P conversion is absent.

Material = P + S + waste +4*(live A+C+I+D). Potential = W +8S +heat.
Both must remain constant, nonnegative and integer. Shared laboratory P/W
banks and contact scheduling remain installed infrastructure; no transport,
compartments, lineage, reproduction, mutation, learning or external supply
appears implicitly. Genesis components are explicitly externally supplied.
New local births have internal event authorship but still descend from those
external ancestors. This gate does not demonstrate de-novo bootstrap.

## Production evidence required of a later simulator

Record pre/post state, immutable catalyst/product IDs and roles, birth/retirement
order, local availability before every proposal, material/work/heat debits,
nutrient provenance and all failures. After retirement, an ID cannot recur.
An externally seeded A0 producing C1 alone does not establish a renewed
production network. A stronger *candidate event chain*, not a life endpoint,
is A0->C1; retire A0; C1->A2; retire C1; A2->C3; C3->I4; I4 processes S.
All births must be paid and their catalysts live at birth. Retirements and
losses count. The chain requires16P,8 construction W and, if all construction
precedes use, at least10 initial W to preserve the2W contact buffer. It does
not establish sustained replacement, heredity, autonomous ecology or a useful
collective descendant. An occupancy/reachability graph alone cannot verify IDs.
