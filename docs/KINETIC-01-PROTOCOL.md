# KINETIC-01 — finite-fuel local catalytic chemistry

Prospective distinct candidate; freeze before any natural-world run. This is an
alternative physical model, not a change to RECYCLE-01 or production Ora physics.
No successful organism, template, catalyst set, encounter order or reward supplied.

## Why change mechanism

RECYCLE-01's blind paid cuts dissipated work, bonds and residual charge; no
capture-paid reconstruction occurred in 288 worlds. Keep that negative intact.
The new candidate couples local generic catalytic rate changes to charged fuel
and spontaneous precursor association. It removes the old separate private-work
payer/constructor/release chain entirely. Catalysts alter probability, never price,
atom supply or energy creation. Components and useful reaction networks must form
from monomers in unscreened local encounters; no organism decisions are scripted.

Random-polymer RAF literature supplies a structural hypothesis, not a dynamic or
energy-accounted demonstration of Ora life. Hordijk & Steel et al., [2016 original
paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.03919), studies structural catalytic sets under
different catalysis distributions. [2015 original boundary analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4333308/)
discusses food-generated closure and spontaneous initial reactions. This candidate
adds finite energy, waste, locality and turnover; it does not reproduce those papers.
Research Pass29 warns that transmission, benefit and external supply are distinct.

## Fixed proposed law and complete accounting

Periodic 4x4 lattice; 96 uniquely identified binary monomer atoms (six per site),
256 individually identifiable charged fuel tokens (16 per site), 512 photon-work
units (32 per site), no polymers. Bits drawn without rejection. Maximum chain
length six; no molecule selected by function or survival. Gross energy 768.

Association consumes two charged fuel tokens -> two spent waste tokens, one bond
potential plus one heat. Atom strings concatenate; product gets fresh ID and both
reactant ancestry. Cleavage at a uniformly drawn existing bond returns the same
atoms in two new objects; one bond potential becomes one heat, no energy refund.
No private work/endowment gifted to a product. Local regeneration converts one
waste token + two photon units -> one charged fuel token + one heat. Diffusion
changes site only. All atom/token ownership, source provenance, photons, bond
energy, heat and exported resource stocks remain authoritative; no hidden top-up.

Generic catalyst mapping is fixed by SHA256 of UTF-8
`KINETIC01|seed|catalystword|leftword|rightword`: first byte <8 means catalytic.
Only chains length >=2 may catalyze; any eligible local chain other than reactants
can be selected uniformly. All 124 possible catalyst types and all 516 ordered
ligation pairs exist prospectively. No table is screened for a working network.
Uncatalyzed association threshold 1/32; mapped-catalyst threshold 8/32 under same
draw. Both pay identical formation prices. Cleavage threshold 1/16, regeneration
threshold 1/2 when affordable; diffusion always on valid selection. These are
programmed candidate physics, not capabilities discovered by Ora.

Exactly 8,192 steps, eight 16-bit draws per step regardless of validity. First
draw mod4 chooses diffusion/association/cleavage/regeneration. Other draws select
site, ordered distinct reactants/catalyst from sorted IDs, cut bond/direction and
reaction threshold modulo its stated denominator; unused draws still consumed.
No resampling invalid attempts. Complete RNG state/cursor, raw requests, resource
ledger, identities, catalyst use, ancestry and every event preserved.

## Fresh paired controls, not favorable founders

Seeds 43000..43031, six arms, 192 worlds / 1,572,864 steps. One same eight-draw tape
per seed across arms; changes in available objects are endogenous, not rematched.

1. Candidate: monomer-only start, local catalyst rate changes, finite regeneration.
2. Inert catalysis: same mapping/opportunities/costs; only uncatalyzed threshold.
3. Well mixed: resource accounting identical; whole-grid candidates replace local
   candidate pools, using same draws. Tests locality, not a rate-success control.
4. Regeneration off: photon stock remains present but inaccessible; no heat or
   energy erased. Tests need for resource recharge separately from organization.
5. Subsidy withdrawal: at step4096 move ALL remaining photon energy to an audited
   exported stock; no subsequent return. Existing paid charged fuel remains as a
   finite buffer. Waste can no longer recharge; measure whether reconstruction
   lasts until that buffer depletes. Do not remove every energy source and then
   treat inevitable inactivity as a discriminating biological failure.
6. Supplied random dimers: before step0 pair the first24 raw atoms without examining
   words or catalyst maps; spend24 fuel, create12 bonds and12 heat with complete
   provenance. All gross resources equal. Supplied organization is assistance and
   cannot count as autonomous origin, even if this arm performs better.

No reserve seeds from prior protocols used. Do not generate these future maps or
seed dynamics during accounting feasibility. First implement and independently
audit the complete law, then commit exact code before executing this frozen panel.
Any clarified draw-layout or serialization detail must be prospectively versioned,
not silently chosen after seeing outcomes.

## Endpoints and falsifiers

Primary world-level endpoint, steps4096..8191: number of distinct catalyst types
that (a) were formed from unsupplied monomers and previously catalyzed a paid
reaction; (b) lost all active copies through recorded cleavage; (c) were reconstructed
by a paid catalyst-assisted association; and (d) subsequently catalyzed another
paid reaction. All four timestamps/IDs/causal catalyst records required. This is
network-mediated component reconstruction, not reproduction or functional inheritance.

Candidate must exhibit that endpoint in >=8 of32 worlds and exceed inert counts
with a one-sided exact paired sign test P<=0.05 on non-tied seed blocks. Report all
zeros, ties and control outcomes; no post-hoc successful-seed selection. Also report
total paid ligations, catalytic uses, decay, depletion times and finite resource
throughput for every arm as secondary engineering/science observables. Increased
formation alone follows the programmed rate change and does not establish organization.

If no complete lifecycle occurs, preserve failure and reject realized reconstruction
under this law. If only supplied dimers work, reject autonomous startup. If repeated
external supply is necessary, reject independent maintenance after withdrawal.
Any atom/fuel/energy/provenance deficit, false catalyst use, RNG divergence or audit
failure invalidates the run, not the hypothesis. No adaptation/evolution claim:
there is no registered reproductive packaging or descendant functional test yet.
