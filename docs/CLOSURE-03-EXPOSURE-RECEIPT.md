## Plain-English run summary

The preceding pilot's cut/sham controls never fired. This run asked whether
that kind of intervention can actually be applied after damage, with live
parents, a matched control and paid resources. It did not ask whether a
population repairs itself without investigator scheduling.

We generated8 fresh development populations (seeds16..23), each in two paired
resource settings:16 post-damage snapshots. Five resource-funded snapshots
supported both controls and30 laboratory probe coupons. Coupons are copies of
recorded states for counterfactual testing, not30 independent populations or
offspring. All unavailable snapshots were retained.

In all30 scheduled coupons, intact/cut/sham triplets spent exactly4 material
and1 fuel per coupon. Five prerequisite-probe births were suppressed by cut;
five matched-product births were suppressed by sham. All ancestry, snapshot,
pair choice, intervention timing and resource checks passed. No coupon counts
as spontaneous repair evidence. One ready snapshot lacked the earlier repair
eligibility; this distinction remains explicit rather than dropping it.

**Meaning:** usable causal controls exist in some post-damage states; the old
absence of control exposure cannot justify an unnecessary-dependency claim.
Missing live producers, missing shams and starvation are distinct limitations.
This small fresh development sample does not diagnose the exact mechanism of
every old pilot failure, establish natural collision frequency, or demonstrate
closed productive organization, inherited function or life.

**Next:** precommit a natural-event study that measures whether relevant births
actually occur, retaining every unexposed/extinct world, before interpreting
restoration contrasts. Select an intervention from structural information at a
declared time; do not impose favorable repair collisions or select successful
populations. Freeze a fresh development panel, exposure denominator, finite
budget/stopping rule and causal comparison before execution. The old reserved
8000..8063 panel remains unexamined. EXPOSURE-01 itself is frozen, not tuned.
