## Finite law and costs

(potential 2). Ten labelled fuel tokens (potential 6, spent potential 0), type
`index % 3`: counts 4,3,3. Initial mechanical work W=0, heat H=8; total energy
68. Supplied ideal shared mechanical coordinate and well-mixed encounter apparatus
are measurement/coupling assumptions. No geometric transport price or empirical
material apparatus price is inferred; results apply only to this declared toy
substrate. Material count stays 13. No supplied active founder or refill.

* Capture: F(type i) -> spent + W2 + H4. Reverse consumes W2 and H4.
  Prefactor 16 if atom i active, otherwise 4. No yield bonus.
* Waste: F -> spent + H6, prefactor 4. Exact reverse H6 -> F.
* Activation: raw i + W3 -> active i (potential 2) + H1.
  Reverse active i + H1 -> raw i + W3. Every activation costs actual work,
  even in controls; reversals refund exactly once.

Each reaction accepts with `prefactor/64 * 2**min(0, delta_H)`;
negative W or H is forbidden. Both directions use the same unchanged cofactor
context. Heat always equals 68-6F-2A-W. Thermal degeneracy is 2**H.
Catalysis changes kinetics only, not equilibrium or attainable energy ceilings.

All 64 six-bit directed off-diagonal catalytic catalogues are included, bit order
(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(1,2),(2,0),(2,1), source then target. No screening or selected
network. Activation encounters one uniformly chosen cofactor. If distinct and
active, candidate prefactor is 64 for its registered edge, otherwise 4.
Independent control always 4. Annealed control uses fresh noise low bit: 64 if
bit < source-row outdegree, otherwise 4. It preserves each row's mean allowance
over its two targets, not occupancy-weighted throughput or fixed connectivity.
Degree-zero/two rows can coincide with candidate; these cases remain included.
Every arm has the same positive basal path, inventories, capture law, cost,
damage, opportunities and endpoint. No control needs joint-catalyst provenance
to succeed. Loss of catalytic acceleration is the independent intervention.
