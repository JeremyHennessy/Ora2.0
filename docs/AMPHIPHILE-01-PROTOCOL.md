# AMPHIPHILE-01: finite component-renewal opportunity audit

Prospective registration, October10,2026. No physical law or population panel.
Preserve MINERAL, COOPERATIVE and all historical studies. This tests a different
coupling: an aggregate's components aid formation of further such components,
rather than a separate converter producing an unconnected chemical product.

## Scoped empirical evidence and our abstraction

[Engwerda et al.,2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7692917/)
reports phase-separated reactants forming surfactant assemblies, with aggregate
growth following a lag. The modified vesicle system degraded too slowly;
supplied DMAP accelerated breakdown. Continuous peroxide regenerated the
head-group precursor; fuel withdrawal led to decay. These observations do not
measure usable-work export, autonomous DMAP production or full resource prices.
We do not reproduce the chemistry or refute the authors' different claim.

Represent hydrophobic thiol H-SH by T; polar thiol D-SH by L;
activated polar disulfide D-S-S-D by B; surfactant H-S-S-D by A;
hydrophobic disulfide H-S-S-H by Z; peroxide by F, water by Q.
Formal transformations, without kinetic or spontaneity claims:

1. T+B -> A+L (component formation).
2. A+T -> Z+L (component breakdown).
3. 2L+F -> B+2Q (head precursor regeneration).

All are atom/moiety balanced, including SH hydrogen, peroxide oxygen and water.
One full cycle sums to 2T+F -> Z+2Q: polar head restored, hydrophobic tail
stock consumed. This is selective regeneration, not full material recycling.
Fresh environmental feed can legitimately pay replacement; material openness
does not disprove self-maintenance or exclude kinetic advantages.

## Frozen enumeration and controls

Before execution pin exact implementation. Enumerate ALL225 initial inventories
T=0..8,B=0..4,F=0..4, with A,L,Z,Q initially zero. No initial working assembly.
Enumerate every reachable monotone accounting state; formation/destruction counts
and phase are tracked. Once at least one A formed, one blind loss consumes A+T
and produces Z+L by the declared breakdown. Require NEW formation afterward.
No refund of lost tail, peroxide, waste or catalyst preparation.
Report repair-attainable case count, minimal supplied inventory witnesses,
maximum total/post-loss formations, full final ledgers and complete graph sizes.
Before and after loss ordinary breakdown remains available. The marked loss
occurs only once; without a pre-loss component the endpoint fails.

Candidate, independent and complete label-shuffled arrangements retain identical
elementary chemistry, feed stocks and endpoint opportunities. No source-derived
rate or free rate multiplier is assigned. In this stoichiometric abstraction
their accessible sets are identical; this cannot decide kinetic benefit.
Unknown reverse kinetics/exergy/transport/buffer/mixing/DMAP formation, renewal
and usable-load prices forbid dynamics or a net-work success score. Maintained
chemical function and mechanical surplus remain separate measured endpoints.

Producer uses memoized exhaustive forward enumeration; independent auditor uses
a separate queue and explicit reaction interpreter with conserved species vectors.
Compare every case and graph census; exact replay; reject altered source,
invented tail regeneration and invented useful-work records. Authored fixtures
must expose a valid minimal repair path and a peroxide-funded regeneration path;
the same paths are available to all three primary arrangements.

Finite operator lock,300-second/CPU,512MiB allocation,4-process limit,
100MiB evidence quota,5GiB free-space floor; preserve outputs, exits, exact pins,
failure evidence and independent C: restoration. No memory-peak claim.

## Decision

Material feasibility alone does not admit a physical world. Stop after this
audit if empirical rates/full useful-work and catalyst-renewal costs remain
unknown. No tuned inventories, selected seeds or substituted favorable yield.
The next discriminator must test aggregate-mediated access versus matched
unassembled/full-shuffle access under independently priced finite feed,
blind damage and new component identity, with catalyst dependence explicit.
