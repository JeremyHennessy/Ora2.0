## Decision and remaining uncertainty

The registered candidate is feasibility-positive: a supplied working template
is not required for these resource-paid authored paths once all three new
possibilities are installed. This does not show that the old law could do it,
that the candidate is realistic chemistry, or that organization naturally emerges.
No toy word is favored by a reward. Yet stable potential, a ready-made assembly
channel, target length, ordered encounters, supplied raw words and initial external
charging remain researcher scaffolding. Resource capture and recharge are installed
abilities; waste is not regenerated. Finite stocks do not demonstrate sustainability.

Second input sequences deliberately repeat the supplied word, even in generic
mode: matching products are not evidence of inherited information. Object ancestry
and paid construction are recorded; independent descendant reproduction, functional
inheritance, adaptation, ecological selection and autonomous maintenance are untested.
Pass29's material transmission/benefit/resupply distinctions remain applicable.
