recovery operate without a named A/C production cycle. All atoms retain external
genesis provenance. A later assembled component containing material recovered
from an earlier internally assembled/degraded component used a nutrient **once,

Active versus paid ghost: conversions were higher in **2/16**, lower in **3/16**,

Global stock access was not universally better: it produced more assemblies but
fewer captures under this geometry. This shortcut changes locality and is not a
trajectory-matched turnover intervention. Direct access is an installed bypass,
not independently generated ability. Neither result is pooled with old models.

The W0 paired regime had zero assembly, recovery and conversions in every arm;
all 128 events/world remained recorded. Funded active had no W0 events, so its
low reuse-use exposure cannot simply be called total work exhaustion.

**All three admission conditions failed:** opportunity in 1/16 rather than >=4,
positive active/ghost conversion differences in 2/16 rather than >=4, pooled
difference -2 rather than positive. **Close this pilot as precommitted.** Do not
extend the horizon, change the prices, reuse samples or run confirmation to obtain
success. Turnover increased component production without demonstrating a usable
resource advantage over the local stock null. This is a limited negative result
under the specified neutral generator, not a universal rejection of recycling.

## Reassessment and next priorities

Paid recycling alone is not the leading organizational mechanism in this regime.
There is installed resource use and one material-reuse lineage, but no independent
maintenance policy, functional reproduction, template transmission or selection.
All assembly consumes reactants, all captured W enters a shared bank, and no
parental sequence causally organizes production of functionally inheriting
descendants. Atom ancestry alone cannot fill that gap.

Next science priority: separately precommit a **generic, energy-accounted
template-dependent production/resource-coupling feasibility test**, against
untemplated/shared-bank and paid ghost/null controls. Check primitive material,
energy, source and ancestry paths before any new world panel. Do not install a
winning sequence, direct group-copying service, fitness/novelty reward, named
cycle or forced cooperation. Templating would be an explicitly installed physical
rule, not an emerged copying ability; useful descendant function needs its own
causal evidence. This is a competing prospective hypothesis, not final architecture
or admission of the closed turnover pilot.

Engineering can use the conserving sequence law as a finite **reference fixture**
for state/noise-cursor/object-ID restart tests, without treating the failed science
candidate as an organism or extending its scientific horizon. HEARTBEAT-02 remains
verified counter continuity only. Off-D backup, physical power-loss/storage-fault
recovery, hardened isolation and separate unattended authorization remain open.
