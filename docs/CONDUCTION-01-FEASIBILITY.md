## Mechanism and question

A finite source drives a conducting path and an ideal back-EMF work transducer.
Can four short joined conductors outperform two simpler continuous conductors
through blind unit-length damage and locally funded replacement?
Kirchhoff series/parallel reduction is sourced from
[MIT circuit notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-01sc-introduction-to-electrical-engineering-and-computer-science-i-spring-2011/3be964f553a1b13301f7899e519bb955_MIT6_01SCS11_lec07_handout.pdf).
This supplies circuit equations, not formation/repair laws or price calibration.
The ideal work-port relationship follows the energy-conserving transducer in
[MIT DC-machine notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-141-modeling-and-simulation-of-dynamic-systems-fall-2006/307d7151ccf4eba25289e17da2a3fe56_dcpmm_basics.pdf).
Resistive heat is not useful output: output is back-EMF times current, the
declared reversible transducer work port. Its realization is an installed ideal
possibility. There is no claim that an actual motor is constructed or controlled.

## Matched apparatus and complete declared cost model

Both arms have two equal length-two paths, four conductor-material units, one
virgin spare unit, the same four damage positions and local unit-patch operation.
The segmented arm has two extra initial interior contacts. The simpler arm's
continuous paths are priced linearly by material length under this registered
abstract fabrication law; they can patch a damaged unit without replacing an
entire cable. Denying that alternative granular repair would manufacture an
advantage. Extra contact resistance is zero in both arms, favoring segmentation;
this is an ideal bound, not measured contact physics.

Declare source voltage6, transducer back-EMF3, source and winding resistance1
each; each unit conductor resistance1. Intact path resistance2, parallel network
resistance1; current1. One unit loss opens a branch, leaving network resistance2
and current3/4. Useful work per slot is3 intact and9/4 damaged; source draw is6
and9/2; heat is3 and9/4. Source draw equals output plus heat in every slot.
The common source/work-port apparatus has declared capital cost q. Conductor
fabrication costs c per material unit, initial contacts cost b per contact,
maintenance costs m per surviving unit per slot, and a unit patch costs c+2b.
All work prices are abstract energy units, not empirical hardware prices.
No recycling credit: removed material becomes scrap, one virgin spare is spent,
patch expenditure becomes waste heat and original formation costs remain sunk.

Both arms receive the same finite startup allowance A=4c+6b+q. They expend
4c+6b+q or4c+4b+q; unused startup work is returned and withdrawn before harvest.
No assistance remains for upkeep/repair. Net external startup debit is provided
minus returned work, counted once as actual capital. A supplied prepared
apparatus and a planned patch are accounting certificates, not autonomous origin.

## Frozen panel and controls

damage positions:972 cases, no seeds. For each, n intact harvest slots, one
damaged slot, a patch from accumulated harvested work only, and two restored
slots. No horizon/resource/cost changes after results. Upkeep follows each
harvest and is debited from stored work; insufficient reserve rejects the
certificate immediately. No planner credit for unfundable bounds. Compare full
life net output and fresh post-damage net output, subtracting capital, upkeep
and patch costs. Source capacity is6(n+3); unused source energy stays unused.
Track supplied startup, returned allowance, capture, heat, maintenance, repair,
virgin/spent/scrap material and final reserve. No double-counted endowment.

Acceptance for a later dynamics protocol requires a segmented advantage in at
least one fundable case for BOTH endpoints, plus a mechanism for autonomous
formation and repair, finite resources, audited paid release, and equally
exposed independent/function-disabled/full-shuffle controls. This admission
test executes only the attainable simpler apparatus comparator; it does not
execute or certify a shuffled natural-world control. If the simpler comparator
dominates, stop before implementing stochastic assembly or running any worlds.
No founder-free, reconstruction or Stage3 acceptance follows from arithmetic.
