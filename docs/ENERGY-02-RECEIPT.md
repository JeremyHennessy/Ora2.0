**Demonstrated:** installed-law physical possibility of fully paid material
construction with actual child food capture, and finite authored renewal after
withdrawal of external activation. Local stored potential removes the prior
simultaneous-work obstruction without increasing private capacity or lowering
copy price. It does not refute the old laws' frozen insufficiency results.

**Not demonstrated:** spontaneous origin, naturally realized reproduction,
functional heredity independent of matching resource supply, self-maintenance,
adaptive selection, ecology, learning or intelligence. Supplied template/bonds,
startup work, compatible food, raw sequences, ordered contacts, zero-cost failed
eligibility checks and stable precursor potential are explicit favorable inputs.
No decay/regeneration, natural contact kinetics or sustained resource renewal
is installed. Current laws require a supplied polymer for biological activation
and templating; a monomer-only genesis does not acquire those abilities by
running longer. Report that structural obstruction rather than sampling an
uninformative neutral panel or retuning prices/capacity until it passes.

Next science gate: precommit a bounded startup/contact/stability feasibility
study that separates supplied organization and external precursor production
from endogenous funding. Assess nonsorted mixed inputs, paid failed encounters,
activation loss and generic startup pathways before any fresh neutral-world
protocol. Keep the full accounting price, nulls and extinction denominators;
new physical assumptions require a separate protocol, not edits to this one.
