## Counts and mathematical reference

For each live pool, enumerate all ordered distinct-ID pairs, count those
producing P, and separately those with an available other token sharing P's
first/last coordinates, different from P/T and both parents. Same endpoint
degrees and four material units do not make that other token functionally
inert. Record all counts and exact rational fractions k/[n(n-1)].

For an idealized sequence of128 independent uniform pair draws from an
unchanging pool, probability of at least one recipe encounter is
1-(1-q)^128. The pool is externally kept static; other products, births,
decay, material competition and ancestry are omitted. With precursor<4,
paid-reference q is0; otherwise it equals recipe q. Fuel caps attempts at128.
This reference is neither a simulation of the installed dynamics nor a
universal upper/lower bound on their success: dynamics can destroy or create
recipes. Uniform draws idealize the implementation's53-bit grid and PRNG.
Do not assign this probability to previously observed histories.

Planning throughput benchmark: at least4/5 probability of one paid recipe
encounter with an available matched token in that static reference. Compare
against this benchmark using exact rational arithmetic. Also report the
minimum independent static draws needed to reach4/5 for each nonzero rate,
even when that exceeds the installed budget. This is an engineering throughput
criterion, not statistical power for restoration or approval of a new law.
Passing cannot admit a dynamic repair study without an independent design
addressing competition, damage eligibility, matching and causal endpoints.

Separately compute the target recipe fraction under independent uniform
genome draws, with replacement over genome types (not token IDs), and its
128-attempt reference. Do not apply this unselected-target calculation to
NATURAL-01's lexicographically selected target or call it an explanation of
that null result. No significance test, parameter search or success filtering.
