# RECTIFY-01: directional conversion versus independent stores

Prospective computational accounting admission, October 10, 2026. No hardware,
biological work, natural worlds or changes to frozen previous laws. Unlike
CONDUCTION segmentation, this candidate changes conversion by nonreciprocal
current paths. Source rationale: [TI design review, section II.B](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/tidu186/tidu186.pdf)
describes a four-diode bridge, conduction on both half waves and forward-voltage
and dynamic-resistance losses. This supplies circuit concepts, not primitive
construction prices or autonomous assembly/repair evidence.

Declare an abstract finite apparatus law: alternating source +6,-6 per cycle,
source resistance1; total storage volume1 with back-EMF3 and resistance1 at
full volume. Storage work capacity128 and initially harvested reserve0; no
initial usable store energy is credited. Source work allowance36 per slot,
2(n+3) slots, unused energy conserved. One-way primitives have forward drop d,
resistance1, reverse conduction0. Their formation costs c and two contacts b;
replacement costs c+2b. Common source/store/installed fabrication capability
capital q=4; fixed setup overhead h per store. These are fully declared abstract
prices, not empirical prices. Realization of these primitives and a constructor
is unverified; do not treat unknown real costs as zero or admit emergence.

Compare bridge four diodes / one store with independent converters k=1,2,4.
All get five diode-material units (four possible initial units plus one spare),
same source, same total store volume and capacity. Independent stores each have
volume1/k and resistance k; k2/k4 split polarity evenly; k1 fixed positive
polarity. No active phase-selection policy. Parallel independent inputs share
the single source resistance; never duplicate environmental source power.
Bridge path has two diodes and full store; independent branches have one diode
and their fractional store. Copper contact resistance is included in the declared
branch resistance; no additional conductor body is secretly charged to controls.
Unknown practical wiring/fabrication costs remain limitations of this abstract law.

Bridge capital4(c+2b)+q+h; independent capital k(c+2b)+q+kh. Both receive a
matched allowance equal to the largest capital across all four arms; unused
allowance is returned before harvesting and charged capital only once. No
startup work remains to subsidize upkeep or replacement. Maintenance m per live
diode and installed store per slot, paid after harvesting; failure stops the
certificate. Every diode loss becomes scrap; one virgin spare is consumed only
when a built diode is damaged and paid replacement succeeds; unbuilt units stay
raw. No recycling credit. Store setup/maintenance and initial capital stay sunk.

For a conducting bridge phase I=(6-3-2d)/4, useful work3I, source draw6I,
heat2dI+4I². For a active independent branches I=(6-3-d)/(1+(k+1)/a), useful
work3I, source draw6I, heat dI+(1+(k+1)/a)I². No active path means zero draw/output.
The number of active independent branches comes from surviving fixed polarities.
Bridge indices0,1 are positive-phase path;2,3 negative. Independent k4 uses the
same indices; k2 indices0 positive,1 negative; k1 index0 positive. Each of four
initial material positions is damaged once; damaging an unbuilt unit leaves
that arm unaffected and incurs no artificial repair cost.

Frozen grid c=(1,2),b=(0,1),m=(1/32,1/8),h=(1,4,8),n=(2,4),d=(1/4,1/2),
damage0..3:384 cases, four arms each. n intact cycles, one damaged cycle,
locally funded scheduled patch if needed, two restored cycles. No seeds or
phase screening. Record full-life net output after capital, upkeep and patch;
post-damage net output after damaged/post upkeep and patch. Keep failures,
all controls and ties. Acceptance for a further circuit audit requires positive
full-life AND post-damage output and superiority on BOTH endpoints over every
fundable independent alternative in at least one case. Failed alternatives may
not be silently excluded: any failure makes that case non-admitting here.

Forward slot ledger and independently derived cycle sums must agree exactly on
all1,536 ledgers; deterministic replay. Even a positive accounting result only
opens a gate for topology/shuffled controls, commutation/transformer alternatives,
explicit contact/constructor physics and autonomous formation/repair registration.
No world dynamics until those gates. Stop if independent alternatives dominate;
do not change costs/horizon on the frozen panel. Prepare no successful founder
for an emergence experiment; prepared scheduled-patch examples are certificates.
