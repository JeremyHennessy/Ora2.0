to waste at no additional work, removes that carrier ID, and never restores
free construction material. This is an authored dissipation rule, not repair.
Ledger: free material+4 per carrier+nutrient+waste; work+8 per nutrient+heat.

## Five arms and honest source matching

- active: functional locally constructed carrier.
- ghost: identical paid physical carrier with function disabled.
- fixed: same physical carrier, readout through explicit fixed presence/function
  table. Active/fixed complete histories must match except arm header.
- no_assembly: construction disabled with no material/work cost. This is an
  unmatched avoided-cost baseline, never a paid sham.
- external_yoked: identical paid ghost; an external conversion mechanism uses
  **exactly the active arm's conversion tick/site schedule**, retrospectively
  recorded from the same genesis/draw stream. It consumes that site's actual
  nutrient and pays all read/processing/construction costs, releases the same
  potential to work/heat, and tags source external. Reject an unaffordable or
  unavailable scheduled conversion rather than topping up. Outside the schedule
  this comparator cannot convert. It is an external laboratory counterfactual,
  not a forecast, independent seed, natural controller or evidence of origin.

No active schedule is fed into other arms or back into active. The yoke only
tests whether declared external source flux reproduces installed access;
do not interpret its equality as endogenous organization. Verify active-yoked
states, timing and costs after explicit functional/source-label normalization.
Ghost/no-assembly trajectories may have different work/constructive totals;
shared draws do not establish globally matched exposure/costs for those arms.
