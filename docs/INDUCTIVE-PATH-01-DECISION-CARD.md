**Question.** Can raw conducting constituents form a renewable current path
that transfers a finite passing magnetic source's energy into retained useful
load energy, outperforming an attainable simpler path on the identical feed?

**Distinctive causal capability and falsifier.** A changing magnetic flux can
induce current without source/load collision. Interacting formed paths could
alter coupling and current decay during a finite encounter. This requires
electrodynamic state and Joule waste, absent from the closed scalar charge,
collision-mode, heat-conduction and capillary proposals. It is not a new charge
screen with different constants. No advantage follows from induction alone:
if one non-reconfiguring path retains as much net work after its full bill,
the multi-path organization is unnecessary and the hypothesis fails.

**Candidate and strongest accessible simpler alternative.** Proposed candidate:
two newly formed, freely moving conducting paths, finite magnetic source and
an identified energy-retaining load. Proposed simpler alternative: one freely
moving, non-reconfiguring path, with unused raw material kept in the same
accessible reserve. Also compare an ordinary direct source/load route wherever
the complete law permits it. Neither comparator is implemented or shown
profitable. A stationary path needs finite reaction-support identity, recoil,
formation and renewal; it cannot be an anchored free coil. Identical source
identities, trajectories, magnetic/kinetic endowment and load opportunity are
required. Do not make a control miss feed that the candidate receives.

**First exact unresolved term.** Phi_path(raw constituent identities, contact
geometry, finite work/thermal stores) must produce a conducting connection
graph and its terminal law R(graph,geometry,T), without an initially finished
coil, soldering machine or connection oracle. The magnetic constitutive
relation, inductance/coupling, carrier-to-material recoil and joint energy
ledger must then follow from that same identified material. Intrinsic raw
conductivity may be constitutive physics; a finished connected circuit is
functional organization, not free raw material. Finite source magnetization
and output storage also need raw formation and causal renewal.

**Concrete way to resolve it.** Require one joint source/constitutive account
of contact formation and energy change, with a terminal conduction assay on
the newly formed path. Destroy an essential conducting connection under a
prospective rule; repeat the assay after formation from fresh identified
constituents. Meter precursor consumption, supplied processing machinery,
Joule heat and new-path identity. If the constructor or load store remains
supplied/unpriced, stop admission. Do not measure a favorable R on a bought
coil and call that raw construction. No numeric R, formation price, efficiency
or performance threshold is chosen while this joint law is absent.
