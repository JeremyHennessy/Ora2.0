# Ora 2.0 seven stage reporting roadmap

VERIFIED = evidence meets the stated scoped criterion; PARTIAL = some required capabilities; CONTROLLED ONLY = supplied conditions or fixtures; EXPERIMENTAL = open investigation without acceptance; NOT DEMONSTRATED = required evidence absent; BLOCKED = specific unmet dependency; NOT ASSESSED = insufficient evidence to determine status.

No completion percentage or confidence score is calculated. Engineering verification does not prove life. These overlapping reporting stages preserve original research gates. The historical October 9 Stage 3 sprint numbered its six categories differently; this edition uses the current user-approved categories exactly.
