# Ora 2.0 complete seven stage progress review

Reviewed October 10, 2026. Reporting categories preserve original experiment gates.

## A. Executive summary

Ora is a working scientific aquarium with separate virtual chemistries, LEGO-like material, finite fuel and independently checked limited wallets. Engineering now preserves finite histories through interruptions, independent-disk backups and new launch identities. The biggest founder-free scientific observation is one fully paid GRADIENT component-renewal lifecycle, although it failed repeatability and comparison gates. The newest controlled advance is BUFFER-01: 60 supplied-apparatus certificates repay startup/upkeep and deliver fresh useful work through a paid replacement contact. Its comparative admission still fails because balanced and loop controls never achieve the complete profitable endpoint in this panel. Ora has not demonstrated integrated autonomous self-maintenance, sustained evolution, independent learning, intelligence or consciousness. Next, Stage 3 needs accessible, fully priced formation and repair with viable simpler controls; recovery and separate learning research can advance in parallel.

**WHAT WE TRIED:** 128 BUFFER cases and five installed apparatus/control arms, finite packets and priced contacts.

**WHAT HAPPENED:** 60 connected certificates repay startup/upkeep and produce fresh replacement output;8 conditional comparisons still fail admission.

**WHY IT MATTERS:** A little machine can pay this toy repair bill; weak controls could mislead.

**WHAT WE LEARNED:** The limited wallet covers whole life/post-loss; useful nulls also need profitable opportunity.

**WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:** Stop this frozen panel; seek accessible formation and viable simpler controls. [BUFFER-01-RECEIPT](../BUFFER-01-RECEIPT.md)

**WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:** Preserve the witness; select a distinct mechanism without tuning the closed panel. [GRADIENT-01-RECEIPT](../GRADIENT-01-RECEIPT.md)
