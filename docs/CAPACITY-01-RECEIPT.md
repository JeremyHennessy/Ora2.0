## What the experiment demonstrated

At unchanged costs, five- and six-component payers can fund complete target
breakup, reactivation, reconstruction, release and subsequent resource capture
after external-bank access closes. This is a programmed physical possibility
realized under a deliberately authored arrangement and encounter sequence.
It does not establish autonomous origin, spontaneous realization, self-maintenance,
reproduction, functional inheritance, adaptation, evolution or intelligence.

The founder-free candidate begins without a product but receives selected raw
bits, external formation work and favorable request ordering. Its payer and
initial target construction are externally funded. The later cut and every new
construction debit must qualify as capture-funded, including the release endowment.
Supplied control pays its complete manufacture transcript before being handed a
product; external control keeps spending its finite bank. These distinctions are
explicit, not reported as discoveries of autonomous organization.
