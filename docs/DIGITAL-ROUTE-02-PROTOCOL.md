# DIGITAL-ROUTE-02 prospective ordinary-route feasibility

Registered October 11, 2026 before new executions. This is a founder-configuration
comparison under the unchanged [ecosystem02 transition law](DIGITAL-ECOSYSTEM-02-CONTRACT.md),
including its reviewed position-formation receipt correction. The closed twelve
ecosystem02 outcomes remain unchanged. This protocol does not extend their seeds,
stocks, horizon or acceptance gates.

Question: can an ordinary paid mobile reproductive program acquire remote food,
produce descendants, and remain exposed to the tick512 change under this law?
Mobility is researcher-supplied comparator assistance, not descendant discovery.

Exact stationary founder triples: `[[0,0,0],[6,0,0],[10,0,0],[11,0,0],[12,0,0],[7,0,0]]`.

are followed by copying until the nursery is complete, attempted division and
MOVE; normal program-counter wrap restarts SENSE. MOVE direction depends on the
last cue parity as already defined. Neither program is screened or selected from
new outcomes. No learned policy, repair, teacher, reward bonus or target descendant
is installed. Faithful inheritance keeps the supplied programs; variation is not
being tested here.

Primary seeds24900..24903, all unscreened, paired across three arms: stationary
faithful, mobile faithful, mobile copy-disabled. Reserved24904..24907 are not
executed in this feasibility block. No historical reserved seed is used. The
same seed gives identical exogenous food identities/cues/stocks; actions and
allocation remain endogenous. Copy-disabled is a birth-causality diagnostic,
not a resource-matched learning control. No shuffled input control.

Each world: material512, food64 packets x20 energy across four sites, founder
energy12, separate constructor128. Each founder assembly pays18 code words and16

All waste, material identity and copy ancestry are recorded. No extra food,
startup fuel, transport subsidy, new schedule or damaged-body assistance.

Stop at1536 global instructions or extinction. Same round-robin scheduler,

Checkpoint64 reconstruction must execute and compare the complete prefix and
continuation. Log all source/config hashes, events, bodies, programs, memory,
resources, births, deaths, copy ancestry and supplied assembly. Independent
interpreter reconstructs the whole transition history and all accounts.

Admission gate A (ordinary route): every mobile faithful primary world must have
at least4 successful remote food acquisitions, at least1 birth caused by a
generation>0 organism, and at least1 successful remote acquisition by a
generation>0 organism. Every copy-disabled world must have0 births. All accounts,
generic positive/impossible fixtures, independent interpretation and restored

Separate exposure gate B: every mobile faithful world must contain at least1
actual ACT attempt on a food packet at tick>=512. A does not imply B. If B fails,
within-life change comparison remains unexposed; do not label failed learning.
Report success/failure/deaths for every seed and founder assistance. No evolution,
learning, repair or new seven-stage acceptance follows from either gate.
Whole/fresh mechanical work and damage are null, not zero estimates.

Native caps:128MiB,5 CPU seconds,10 wall seconds,3 processes, stdout1MiB per call.
Exclusive existing operator lock, disk floor5GiB, evidence64MiB total with16MiB
each for raw/archive/restore/failure. Preserve native failures and timeouts;
require post-close owned processes exited. Archive reviewed source, restore it,
rerun exact bounded histories and separately audit the sealed evidence ledger.

Decision after this block: A fails -> record the exact ordinary-route limitation;
A passes/B fails -> ordinary route available, define the particular missing
exposure dependency prospectively; both pass -> freeze claim-specific evolutionary
or own-experience controls before testing those claims. No persistent activation.
