## Distinct mechanism considered

Conductive deposition couples evolving geometry to electric-field-driven growth.
The [2021 experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8617192/) uses prepared
gold electrodes, EDOT/benzoquinone/electrolyte and externally generated signals.
Its public [dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.16814710.v1) contains
electrical characterizations, growth-image measurements and compact-circuit data.
These establish controlled growth/response, not autonomous energy acquisition.

The [2022 model](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-10082-6) feeds prescribed
electrode potentials into geometry-dependent particle motion and attachment.
It maintains particle concentration, permanently attaches particles, omits
voltage drop along deposited conductors and explicitly lacks quantitative
one-to-one mapping to experiment. It cannot be installed as a finite energy
ledger without a distinct, prospectively specified law.

The [copper nanopore study](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep07056) supports
surface-charge-dependent transport during powered deposition. Its prepared
charged membrane and electrical source would be apparatus/endowment costs.
Faster current or growth does not by itself establish usable-work surplus.

## Complete numerical-file check

Only file31090411, 2,608,639 bytes, was downloaded. Publisher MD5 and recorded
SHA256 agree; original bytes remain preserved. All9,485 rows have60 columns.
Two separate parsing methods agree on17 section positions and row count.
Three sections label measured current: cyclic voltammetry, DC measurements
during the growth study, and supplementary DC characterization. The sole
section with time and current labels the current **simulated** (Figure5b).
This CSV does not identify measured same-run growth voltage/current/time needed
to integrate actual construction input. Growth times in DC header labels are
sample ages, not a continuous construction-current record.

The initial wrapper completed acquisition/hash checks then failed its UTF-8
preview. That failure is preserved. A reversible Latin-1 byte view enabled
inspection without rewriting or redownloading the source. No encoding claim
about the authors, fitted rates, energy calculation, source-code execution or
new world run. Schema agreement is not independent experimental reproduction.
Supplementary PDF/video were not inspected; absence here is not universal absence.

## Decision

**NOT_ADMITTED_MISSING_GROWTH_ENERGY_AND_REPAIR_LOAD_ACCOUNTING.** Keep this
candidate uncalibrated. This is an admission failure, not a physical impossibility
proof or an unsuccessful Ora dynamics panel. Unknown source/preparation prices,
reverse chemistry, usable-load coupling and repair cost remain unknown.

Do not substitute simulated RC current, conductance, morphology or voltage for
measured complete growth energy. Do not install a favorable work yield or call
powered construction self-maintenance. No change to historical protocols or seeds.

The next decisive experiment still requires fully determined finite source and
material ledgers, explicit load output, blind damage with fresh reconstruction
provenance, both whole-life and post-damage surplus, withdrawal/reserve accounting
and attainable resource-matched independent/full-shuffle controls. A sourced
law or an explicitly new dimensionless law must pass that admission before
unscreened sampling. Repeat source retrieval only when relevant evidence changes.
Stage3 acceptance, observer publication pause and persistent-pilot gates unchanged.
