## Why repeated reproduction and actual supply still matter

Simple grouped identities expose the tradeoff without running a lineage:

- Nine successful feedback cycles and one paid division cost `9×6 + 27 + 30 = 111` parent energy while nine captures provide 180. Surplus energy can become cap heat; it is not all retained benefit.
- Eighteen ordinary blind cycles, eighteen paid COPY groups and two paid divisions cost `18×5 + 54 + 60 = 204`, while nine captures provide 180. This deficit requires additional intake or reserve; it does **not** prove the blind policy cannot reproduce repeatedly. Once a nursery is full, two additional blind cycles cost ten dispatches, omit extra COPY charges and can add one 20-unit capture under the stable-food premise. Phase, refusal and survival guards still matter.
- A one-site stationary population cannot assume unlimited forage success: renewable input is at most `4×20/64 = 1.25` energy per global dispatch before rejected packets and failed captures. The feedback routine's hypothetical one capture per six own dispatches is a demand assumption, not an arrival guarantee. Births and competition change the mapping between global and personal time.

For a fixed 27-word focal lineage, let `D` be its dispatch count, `B` its completed births, `T` global dispatch time and `I` the initial food stock at its one site. If all descendants stay at that site, no outsider supplies energy, and the only accessible initial organism energy is the founder's 12, energy conservation gives the deliberately loose bound:

```text
D + 45 B <= 12 + 20 [ I + 4 floor(T/64) ]
```

Each completed birth needs at least 27 extra COPY heat and 18 division heat beyond dispatch charges; the child's 12 energy is an internal transfer, not newly created lineage input. Unfinished copying, overflow, remaining living energy and deaths can only tighten the bound. Constructor reserves are not a foraging subsidy. Occupancy limits/rejected offers and target errors reduce attainable intake further.

Only when **every** dispatch belongs to that one stationary lineage may `D=T` be used. This specialization is not applied to the actual mobile/mutating histories. At the source's finite 4,096-step horizon, site 0 then has an upper bound of 37 completed births, not a prediction or demonstrated capacity. Separating initial stock yields a renewal-surplus coefficient of `1/180` births per global dispatch; this is algebra in a finite-horizon bound, not a measured steady rate, infinite-horizon qualification or continuous-world result. The other sites' initial stocks and all bound rows are retained.

## Decision and the next scientific gate

**VERIFIED within scope:** exact old-data custody and category/counter reconciliation; legal equal-length controls; conditional first-child accounting; source supply inequalities under explicit assumptions. There are real repeated-target opportunities in the closed environment.

**NOT YET PROVEN:** that a feasible organism can retain/use the relevant information more cheaply than strong blind alternatives, gain a paid lifetime/descendant advantage under real supply and interruptions, or arise through viable mutation intermediates. No new learning algorithm, selected improvement, inherited acquired knowledge or intelligence was demonstrated. A local port register is experience state; it is not an implemented older-site memory mechanism.

**Decision: ecological superiority UNKNOWN; mutation feasibility UNKNOWN; population NO-GO.** Do not launch merely because most observed targets repeat, or treat a positive conditional surplus as an execution gate passing. Do not scale population size, increase mutation dose or tune a new algorithm to reverse this finding.

The highest-value next gate is an independent prospective feasibility review of a **food- and opportunity-matched lifetime comparison**, including both fixed ports, the same-length blind alternator and a cheap nursery-aware blind control; count every death, empty encounter, copying cost and viable descendant. Before execution it also needs an actual reachable information contrast, a viable mutation path, frozen metrics/budgets/controls and the existing registration/native ownership/resource gates. If these cannot be justified, retain NO-GO rather than spend another population bank. This report is preparation, not registration or launch authorization.
