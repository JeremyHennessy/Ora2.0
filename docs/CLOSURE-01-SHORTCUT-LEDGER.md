## Most important negative observation

The **cell-like appearance** of a ring has *less* evidence than the much simpler description: local autocatalytic conversion in a reaction-diffusion field. The “no shell synthesis” control increases core repair, while the “M cannot affect movement” control gives a similar core outcome to intact. This may indicate resource/opportunity costs and weak shell function under our rule; it **does not establish** which alternative explains everything without a cost- and flux-matched follow-up.

**Do not declare:** a new organism, newly created biological life, self-invented chemistry, emergent intelligence, independent individuality, virtual consciousness, autopoiesis, or self-generated computational language.

## Evidence-based response

1. Preserve raw worlds, frame traces and checksums for all origins and failed cases. Do not retune CLOSURE-01.
2. If we stay with this substrate, preregister a *different* experiment using a cost-matched ghost-shell control and normalized boundary-crossing flux, without M in the outcome label.
3. If even this control fails to show functional maintenance, **retire the shell-based membrane hypothesis** and compare against an independent causal process-organization substrate.
4. Never stitch together the partial successes of separate AL02 inheritance and CLOSURE-01 catalytic patches to declare a single self-replicating protocell.
5. Any future claim of organismhood must enumerate which constraints/affordances were designed, and what specific mechanisms were actually generated or inherited by the simulated world.
