* Capture: two local photon tokens become one charged carrier plus one heat.
* Binding: one endpoint pays two of its carriers; one bond potential plus one heat.
  Payer choice is blind, no affordable-endpoint fallback. Fresh bond ID records both
  endpoints and consumed carrier IDs; bond assistance inherits carrier assistance.
* Unbinding: one bond potential becomes one heat; two ports released, no refund.
* Transport: a selected existing bond conveys energy from blindly chosen donor to
  its other endpoint, consuming two donor carriers to create one receiver carrier
  plus one heat, only if donor>=2 and receiver<8. New carrier has both parents and
  complete bond/time route ancestry. No charge teleport or endowment.
* Supplied bonds: an external actuator can establish a neighboring link for four
  external-work units -> one bond potential plus three heat, zero private charge.
  This idealized global external actuator is explicit paid assistance, not a
  discovered internal delivery mechanism. No atoms transported or free port supplied.
